# Real Estate Agency CRM: Properties, Matching and Auto-Posting to Portals in One System

A realtor works not with an abstract deal, but with a bundle of client, property, showing and portal listing. A regular CRM tracks the lead and the deal, but does not store a property database, cannot match options to a request and does not post them to Cian, Avito and Domclick. We select and set up CRM specifically for agency processes, so that properties, matching, pipeline and publishing live in one system instead of Excel and a dozen tabs. This is part of our [CRM implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/) practice: first we study your processes, then we configure the system around them.

## Why a real estate agency needs a CRM

A real estate CRM is a system that ties together the four entities of the realtor business: client, property, deal and portal listing. This exact bundle is what sets it apart from a universal CRM. A regular system moves a lead through the pipeline and stores contacts, but it has no property card with photos, layout, price and status, just as it has no matching of options to a buyer's request.

In practice this means that in an industry CRM the same property is visible in the database, linked to the right clients through matching, moves through its own deal pipeline and goes out as a listing to portals all at once. The manager no longer keeps these links in their head or moves data by hand between systems.

## What a realtor loses without a CRM

While the property and client base lives in spreadsheets and messengers, an agency loses money at three points. Requests from portals and calls get forgotten or handled late, and the client leaves for whoever answered first. The property base goes stale: the price changed, the property went on reserve, but the listing still hangs and generates empty calls. Publishing eats up hours, because every property has to be entered manually into Avito, Cian, Domclick and Yandex.Realty one by one.

Auto-posting through a single XML feed removes exactly the third loss. By the estimate of industry integrators, it [saves around 40–60 minutes per property](https://smartagent.ru) versus manual duplication across all portals. On a base of a hundred active properties that is dozens of hours a month that go into copy-paste instead of showings and deals.

## Real estate CRM features: properties, matching, unit grid, feed

What sets an industry CRM apart from a universal one is the feature set built for real estate work. The baseline profile is this:

- **Property database.** The card stores photos, layouts, address, area, floor, price, commission, status (available, reserved, sold) and price change history.
- **Matching by request.** The system itself suggests properties for the client's parameters and records what was shown and to whom.
- **New-build unit grid.** A visual grid of apartments by building and floor with statuses for work with developers.
- **Pipelines by deal type.** Separate pipelines for purchase, sale, new builds, resale and commercial, instead of one for everything.
- **Auto-posting.** A single feed publishes properties to portals and updates them when the price or status changes.
- **Request intake from portals.** Listing inquiries and calls land in the CRM as leads and are not lost.

## How the deal pipeline works in real estate

A deal at an agency is longer than in retail and runs through eight predictable stages. It is convenient to track them in a separate pipeline, to see exactly where clients get stuck.

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/crm-dlya-nedvizhimosti-1.svg | Real estate deal pipeline: 8 stages from contact to close]

The client journey looks like this: first contact, qualification, property matching, showing, negotiation, deposit, deal, close. For purchase, sale, new builds and resale the stage logic differs, so in the CRM you set up several pipelines instead of trying to fit everything into one. Then the manager sees the conversion of each stage and understands whether clients are lost at matching, at showings or already at negotiation.

## Which CRM suits a real estate agency

The choice comes down to a fork: a dedicated real estate CRM or a universal platform with an industry module. Dedicated solutions have the property database and matching built in out of the box. Universal platforms like Bitrix24 and amoCRM are strong in analytics, telephony and tasks, and add property work through industry add-ons.

| Criterion | Dedicated CRM | Bitrix24 | amoCRM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Property database and matching | Built in out of the box | Through an industry module | Through an industry add-on |
| New-build unit grid | Usually present | Through a module | Through an add-on |
| Auto-posting to portals | Part of the profile | Configurable | Configurable |
| Analytics, telephony, tasks | Basic | Strong | Strong |
| Flexibility for other processes | Limited to the niche | High | High |

A dedicated system closes niche tasks faster, while a universal platform grows better with the agency as departments, cross-cutting reports and adjacent processes appear. If you are already looking at a specific platform, their capabilities are covered in more depth on the [Bitrix24 implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/bitrix24/) and [amoCRM implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/amocrm/) pages. ## Integration with portals: Cian, Avito, Domclick, Yandex.Realty

Auto-posting rests on a single XML feed. You enter a property once in the CRM, and the system builds the feed and publishes the listing to the connected portals: Cian, Avito, Domclick, Yandex.Realty and others. The exact set depends on the region and often includes Yula and Mail.ru Realty. When the price or status of a property changes, the listings update without manual edits on each site.

[IMG: ../assets/images/crm-dlya-nedvizhimosti-1.jpg | Auto-posting listings from CRM to Cian, Avito, Domclick and Yandex.Realty through an XML feed]

The link works in the other direction too. Listing messages and calls from portals are relayed into the CRM as requests and reach the responsible manager. That way an inquiry from Avito or Cian is not lost in personal chats, but immediately becomes a lead with history and a reminder.

## FAQ

**How is a real estate CRM different from a regular CRM?**
The key difference is the bundle of client, property, deal and portal listing. A regular CRM tracks the lead and the deal, but does not store a property database with photos, layout and status, and cannot match options to a request. An industry CRM keeps properties, matching, pipeline and auto-posting in one system.

**Will amoCRM or Bitrix24 work for a real estate agency, or is a dedicated CRM needed?**
Both options work. Bitrix24 and amoCRM cover property work through industry modules and add-ons and are strong in analytics, telephony and tasks. A dedicated CRM gives the property database and matching out of the box. Which is more worthwhile for you depends on agency size and growth plans, and that is what we work through in the audit.

**Can auto-posting to Cian, Avito, Domclick and Yandex.Realty be set up?**
Yes. A property is entered in the CRM once, and the system builds a single XML feed and publishes the listings to the connected portals. When the price or status changes, the listings update automatically, and inquiries on them come back into the CRM as requests.

**How does property matching by client request work in the CRM?**
You record the client's parameters, and the system suggests suitable properties from the database and marks what was shown and to whom. That way the manager does not keep options in their head and does not lose the matching history when a client is handed to a colleague.

**What is a new-build unit grid and does our agency need it?**
The unit grid is a visual grid of apartments by building and floor with statuses of available, reserved, sold. It is needed by agencies that work with developers and new builds. For pure resale you can do without it.

**How much does turnkey real estate agency CRM implementation cost?**
A simple launch starts [from 50,000 RUB](https://mmp-group.ru). For an agency of 5–10 realtors the market benchmark is [400–600k RUB](https://falconspace.ru), and for a group of companies with a complex structure [around 900k RUB](https://falconspace.ru). We lock the exact estimate after the process audit.

**How long does implementation take?**
A pilot on a group of 5–10 users [takes around 2–3 weeks](https://semantiqo.ru). A full project for an agency of 5–10 realtors runs [3–4 months](https://falconspace.ru), and for a large group of companies it can reach [up to 8 months](https://falconspace.ru).

**Will the CRM work for commercial real estate and rentals, not just sales?**
Yes. For purchase, sale, rental and commercial, separate pipelines with their own stage logic are set up, and the property card is configured for the needed type. One tool covers the agency's different lines of business.

**Can the property and client base be migrated from Excel or an old system?**
Yes, data migration is a standard part of implementation. We take properties, clients and history from Excel or the previous CRM, clean and consolidate them, so you start on your own base instead of filling the system in from scratch.

**How does the CRM help avoid losing requests from portals and calls?**
Listing inquiries and inbound calls land in the CRM as leads with a responsible manager and a reminder. A request does not settle in a personal chat or in missed calls, but moves through the pipeline. It is exactly on lost requests that agencies most often lose money.

**Will the solution work for a private realtor or only a large agency?**
It will work for a private realtor too. For a single specialist or a small team we assemble a light configuration with a property database, matching and auto-posting without extra modules. The logic of such a launch is described in the article on [CRM for small business](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/crm-dlya-malogo-biznesa/).

**Are you tied to one CRM or do you select for our processes?**
We are an independent integrator and not tied to a single vendor. First we study your processes, then we select the system that fits them. If a light solution is enough, we will offer it rather than the most expensive project.
