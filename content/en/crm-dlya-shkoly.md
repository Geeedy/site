# CRM for a School and Learning Center: Students, Schedule, Payments, and Reminders in One System

A private school, a learning center, language or dance courses do not live on deals; they live on students, groups, and the schedule. A regular CRM leads a lead from request to sale, but it does not know what a group, a membership, or a trial lesson is. We pick and set up a system that keeps students, the schedule, payments, and reminders to parents in one place, so requests are not lost and debts and renewals no longer have to be counted by hand. This is part of our approach to [CRM implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/): we study your processes and pick a platform to fit them, not the other way around.

## Why a school and learning center needs CRM

CRM for a school is a system that leads not abstract deals, but the core link of student, group, schedule, and payment or membership. A regular all-purpose CRM has a lead, a contact, and a deal, but it has no learning module with groups, class schedule, memberships, and attendance out of the box ([SalesAP](https://salesap.ru/), 2024; [a2is.ru](https://www.a2is.ru/), 2026). This learning layer is exactly what sets an education solution apart from an all-purpose sales tool, and it decides whether the administrator works in a single window or once again keeps a notebook and Excel next to the CRM.

## What a school loses without CRM

While records are scattered across messengers, spreadsheets, and the administrator's head, money leaks in predictable places. A request from the website or from an ad arrives and never reaches a call. A parent signed up for a trial and did not show up because no one reminded them. A membership ran out and no one offered a renewal. A payment came in, but no one marked it in the spreadsheet, and the debt is noticed a month later. An industry CRM closes these gaps with a set of core modules: groups and schedule, request capture and sales pipeline, finance and payments, tasks and reminders, a student database, automatic membership renewal, messenger integrations, contract templates, and e-document flow ([a2is.ru](https://www.a2is.ru/), 2026).

## CRM features for a school: students, groups, schedule, memberships, reminders

The industry feature set is built around the real day of the administrator and the teacher.

- **Student and parent database.** A card with the history of visits, payments, group, and parent contacts.
- **Groups and schedule.** A student is tied to a group, a group to a schedule and a teacher, classes are visible in a shared calendar.
- **Memberships and one-off payments.** The system counts the remaining classes, records payment status, and highlights debts.
- **Enrollment pipeline.** A request is led through stages from first contact to transfer into a permanent group.
- **Automatic reminders.** A message about the class and the payment goes to the parent without the administrator.
- **Attendance.** Marking attendance in class deducts a class from the membership and lands in the report to the manager.

## How the trial-lesson enrollment pipeline works

Enrolling in a school is not one deal, but a chain of steps where clients are lost at each one. CRM captures requests from website forms, ads, and messengers automatically, so they do not vanish, and leads the parent through the stages ([frigat.net](https://frigat.net/), 2026; [templjoomla.ru](https://templjoomla.ru/), 2026).

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/crm-dlya-shkoly-1.svg | School enrollment pipeline: 7 stages from request to moving a student into a group]

The path looks like this. A request from the website, an ad, or a messenger lands in the CRM. The administrator contacts the parent. The child is booked for a trial lesson. The parent and child come for the visit. After the trial, a membership offer is made. The parent makes a payment. The student is moved into a permanent group. At each step the system assigns a task to the responsible person and reminds them if the stage stalls, so the request is not forgotten between the call and the visit.

## Which CRM suits a school: all-purpose, industry, or GetCourse

The choice comes down to three options, and the right one depends on what sits at the center of your business: sales, the learning process, or online education.

| Criterion | All-purpose (Bitrix24, amoCRM) | Industry for a learning center | GetCourse (online) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strong in | sales, tasks, telephony | groups, schedule, memberships | courses, access, webinars |
| Learning module | added by configuration | out of the box | teaching, not sales |
| Who it suits | focus on sales and general automation | offline school with groups and a schedule | online school and courses |

All-purpose [Bitrix24](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/bitrix24/) and [amoCRM](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/amocrm/) are good when general automation and sales matter more, and the learning layer is built up by configuration or an industry add-on. Industry solutions for education in Russia, among them AlfaCRM, HolliHop, 2Kurs, and KeyCRM, give groups and schedule right away ([spark.ru](https://spark.ru/), 2025; [a2is.ru](https://www.a2is.ru/), 2026). If the school is small, look separately at the breakdown for [CRM for small business](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/crm-dlya-malogo-biznesa/), where the solution is chosen by size rather than by industry.

## Integrations: website, online payment, telephony, messengers

CRM is useful exactly as far as it is connected to the school's other channels.

- **Website forms.** Trial and callback requests drop into the CRM automatically, without manual transfer.
- **Online payment and installments.** Acquiring and installments tie the payment to the student and the membership.
- **IP telephony.** Calls are recorded and land in the card, missed ones turn into a task to call back.
- **Messengers.** Reminders and correspondence with parents run through WhatsApp and Telegram from one system.

## FAQ

**How is CRM for a school different from a regular CRM?**
A regular CRM leads a lead and a deal, while CRM for a school has a learning module: groups, schedule, memberships, and attendance. The core link is student, group, schedule, and payment. Without the learning layer the administrator will once again keep Excel next to the CRM.

**Will amoCRM or Bitrix24 suit a school, or do you need an industry CRM?**
Both options work. All-purpose [Bitrix24](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/bitrix24/) and [amoCRM](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/amocrm/) suit you when the focus is on sales and general automation, and the learning module is built up by configuration. An industry CRM gives groups, schedule, and memberships right away. A process audit will show which is more worthwhile for you.

**How does the CRM handle students, groups, and schedule?**
A student is tied to a group, a group to a schedule and a teacher. The student card shows visits, payments, and the remaining membership balance, and classes appear in a shared calendar. Marking attendance in class deducts a lesson from the membership and lands in the report to the manager.

**Can you run memberships and one-off payments and control debts?**
Yes. The system counts the remaining classes on the membership, records payment status, and highlights outstanding debt. The CRM links the request, the invoice, and the payment status and notifies the responsible people, so manual monitoring of incoming payments and debts goes away.

**How does the CRM help avoid losing trial-lesson requests?**
Requests from website forms, ads, and messengers land in the CRM automatically, without manual transfer. The system assigns a task to contact the parent and reminds you if a stage stalls. This way the request is not forgotten between first contact and the visit.

**Can you set up automatic reminders to parents about the class and payment?**
Yes. Standard reminders go out a day before the lesson, a couple of days before payment, and a week after the first lesson. Sending runs by SMS, email, and in messengers, and no administrator involvement is required.

**We have an online school on GetCourse, how do we link it with CRM?**
Roles are split. GetCourse leads the teaching, courses, access, and personal accounts, while the CRM leads sales and the pipeline. Course access opens automatically after payment, so the sales team works in its usual CRM, and the student gets materials without a manual grant.

**Will the solution suit a language, dance, or children's school, not only courses?**
Yes. The logic of student, group, schedule, and membership is the same for a language, dance, or music school and children's courses. The reference data and scenarios change, not the model itself. We set the system up for the format of your classes.

**How much does turnkey CRM implementation for a school cost?**
For a small center up to 100 students implementation runs [50,000–150,000 RUB](https://spark.ru/), for a medium school of 100–500 students [150,000–500,000 RUB](https://spark.ru/), then a subscription from 5,000 RUB/month ([spark.ru](https://spark.ru/), 2025). The exact sum depends on the number of integrations and the state of the data, and we lock the estimate after the audit.

**How long does implementation take?**
Basic setup takes [2–4 weeks](https://elma365.com/), a full project [1.5–3 months](https://i-seo.pro/), a complex implementation with many integrations [3–6 months](https://elma365.com/). The timeline is made up of analysis, setup, integrations, testing, and team training.

**Can you migrate the student and payment database from Excel or an old system?**
Yes. Migrating the database is a standard part of implementation. We take students, groups, payment history, and memberships from Excel or the previous system, clean and consolidate the data, so you start in the new CRM without losses.
