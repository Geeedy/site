# Sales Automation: Sales Team Processes Without Manual Work

Requests get lost between the website, messengers, and calls. Managers enter leads by hand, forget to call back, treat "hot" and "cold" the same way, and reports get pulled together in Excel on Fridays. We take the manual work out of the funnel: we set up request auto-capture, routing, tasks, triggers, AI scoring, and documents so the manager handles negotiations instead of moving data around. McKinsey estimates that about [30% of sales tasks are technically automatable](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/sales-automation-the-key-to-boosting-revenue-and-reducing-costs) with existing technology, and we hand that routine off to automatic rules.

## What sales automation is

Sales automation moves routine funnel steps (request capture, routing, tasks, follow-up, documents, reports) onto automatic rules and AI, so the team spends its time on negotiations instead of manual entry. It is important not to confuse this with installing the system itself. A CRM works as a storage program for deals and contacts, while automation configures the processes inside it that move a deal without human involvement.

Put simply, [CRM implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/) answers the question "what does the sales team work in," while automation answers "what happens inside that system on its own." You can install a CRM and keep doing everything by hand. The value appears when the funnel accepts a request on its own, sets a task, and reminds you about an overdue item.

## Which sales processes we automate

We automate the funnel steps where the manager works by hand today and loses time or requests. The basic set of processes looks like this:

1. **Lead auto-capture.** Requests from the website, forms, messengers, calls, and email land in a single funnel with no manual transfer.
2. **Routing to managers.** A lead goes to the right employee by rules: queue, region, product, workload.
3. **Auto-tasks and reminders.** The system sets "call back," "send a proposal," "remind about payment" on its own and keeps deadlines.
4. **Triggers and robots.** A deal changes status and this launches emails and notifications based on events.
5. **AI lead scoring.** Requests are ranked by purchase probability so "hot" ones get picked up first.
6. **Auto-documents.** Proposals, invoices, and contracts are generated from a template using deal data.
7. **Automatic reporting.** The funnel, stage-by-stage conversion, and manager workload are calculated without Excel.

We do not switch everything on at once. At the audit we look at where routine eats the most time and automate that first.

## How the funnel works: triggers and robots

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/avtomatizaciya-prodazh-1.svg | Automated sales funnel flow: request, auto-capture, AI scoring, auto-tasks, robots, document, deal]

The heart of automation is triggers and robots. These are rules of the form "if an event happens, perform an action." Per platform documentation, robots automatically change a deal's status, set tasks, send reminders, and notify the manager about overdue items without the sales rep's involvement ([bitrix24.ru](https://www.bitrix24.ru/features/robots.php), 2026; [amocrm.ru](https://www.amocrm.ru/features/pipeline-management), 2026).

In practice it looks like this. A deal moves to the "invoice issued" stage and the robot sets a task on its own to check the payment in two days. If there is no payment, an escalation fires: a notification to the manager. A deal "stalls" without activity for a week and a revival scenario for "sleeping" deals starts: an email to the client and a reminder to the sales rep. A person does not hold this in their head, and the system never forgets.

*From our practice.* We always draw a process map before configuring robots. A common mistake in do-it-yourself automation is trying to turn everything on at once. Robots start sending clients excessive emails, the funnel clogs with duplicate tasks, and managers switch off notifications. We launch scenarios one at a time and test each on real deals.

## AI lead scoring and AI agents in sales

When there are many requests, the key question becomes who to handle first. AI scoring ranks leads by purchase probability based on deal history and client behavior, so managers pick up the "hot" ones first and do not waste time on non-target requests ([hubspot.com](https://www.hubspot.com/products/sales/lead-scoring), 2026; [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/marketing), 2026). This lifts funnel conversion, because the team's effort concentrates where the chance of a deal is higher.

Response speed matters here no less than priority. Per the classic Lead Response Management study, contacting a lead [within 5 minutes versus 30 minutes raises the chance of qualification by roughly 21 times](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads). A robot and AI answer instantly at any hour, whereas a human physically cannot keep up.

A separate level is set by an AI agent that does not just score but runs a dialogue. An [AI agent for sales](/uslugi/ii-agenty/agenty-dlya-prodazh/) qualifies a request, answers the first questions 24/7, books a meeting, and hands a "hot" lead to the manager. This is a separate product that embeds into the automated funnel. For inbound requests in messengers, the same primary-qualification role is covered by a [GPT-based chatbot for lead qualification](/uslugi/chat-boty/bot-na-gpt/). Within this service we configure the scoring and lead handoff points, while the depth of dialogue scenarios is covered on the dedicated pages.

## Integrations: telephony, messengers, website, 1C

- **Website and forms.** A request drops into the funnel with a source tag and UTM.
- **Messengers.** WhatsApp and Telegram are handled from the deal card, and the chat is not lost.
- **Telephony.** Calls are written into the card, and a missed call turns into a task.
- **1C.** Invoices, payments, and shipment statuses sync between sales and accounting, which covers the "1C sales automation" request without double entry ([1c.ru](https://1c.ru/), 2026).

## FAQ
**How is sales automation different from CRM implementation?**
CRM implementation means installing and configuring the system itself as a store of deals and contacts. Automation configures the funnel processes inside it: request capture, routing, tasks, triggers, scoring, documents, reports. You can install a CRM and keep doing everything by hand; automation removes the manual work.

**Which sales team processes can be automated first?**
Usually we start with request auto-capture and routing to managers, because that is where the most leads are lost. Next we add auto-tasks, triggers, and robots, then AI scoring and auto-documents. We set the specific order at the audit, based on where routine eats the most time.

**How does lead auto-capture across all channels work?**
Requests from the website, forms, messengers, calls, and email automatically land in a single funnel with a source tag, without manual transfer. This reduces the loss of requests "between channels," when an inquiry arrives on WhatsApp and the manager never checked it. Every request immediately becomes a deal with a task.

**What are triggers and robots in a sales funnel?**
These are rules of the form "if an event happens, perform an action." A robot changes the deal status on its own, sets tasks, sends reminders, and notifies the manager about overdue items without the sales rep's involvement. For example, on the move to the "invoice" stage the system sets a task to check payment, and on a stall it launches deal revival.

**How does AI scoring identify "hot" leads?**
AI analyzes the history of your deals and the client's behavior, then ranks requests by purchase probability. Managers pick up the "hot" ones first and do not waste time on non-target inquiries, which lifts funnel conversion. Scoring works more accurately once history accumulates in the CRM.

**How is an AI agent in sales different from CRM robots?**
Robots execute rigid rules based on events: change a status, set a task, send a template. An [AI agent for sales](/uslugi/ii-agenty/agenty-dlya-prodazh/) runs a live dialogue: it qualifies a request, answers questions 24/7, books a meeting, and hands a hot lead to the manager. This is a separate product that embeds into the automated funnel.

**Can issuing proposals, invoices, and contracts be automated?**
Yes. The CRM generates commercial proposals, invoices, and contracts from a template using deal data, without manual filling. This shortens the deal cycle and removes errors such as wrong details or amounts. The manager only needs to check and send the document to the client.

**How does sales automation connect to telephony and messengers?**
We connect telephony, WhatsApp, and Telegram directly into the CRM. Calls are written into the deal card, a missed call turns into a task, and chat is handled from the system's interface. The manager works from one window, and the head sees all communication on the deal.

**Is sales automation on top of 1C possible?**
Yes. We configure an exchange between the CRM and 1C: invoices, payments, and shipment statuses sync without double entry. This covers the "1C sales automation" request, when accounting lives in 1C and sales are run in the CRM. The sales team sees payment status without entering the accounting program.

**Which CRM is better for automation, Bitrix24 or amoCRM?**
It depends on your goals. [Automation in Bitrix24](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/bitrix24/) is strong when you need whole-company processes, documents, and a portal. A [funnel in amoCRM](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/amocrm/) wins on speed and messengers for short deals. We implement both platforms and recommend in writing, with a rationale for your processes.

We calculate the exact cost after the audit, because the scope depends on the number of processes and channels. The estimate is locked before kickoff and does not grow during the work.

**What effect does sales team automation give, and over what period?**
