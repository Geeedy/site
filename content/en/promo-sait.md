# Promo Website Development for a Campaign, Product Launch and Event

A promo website is needed when a campaign has a bold creative message and a hard deadline: a new product launch, an ad campaign, a contest or a brand anniversary. It is a temporary web project that lives for the run of the campaign and bets on emotion, not just on a request form. We build such sites richer than an ordinary one-pager, through animation, interactivity and storytelling, so the visitor walks through the product story and remembers the brand. This is one of the directions within [website development](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/): we pick the format for the task rather than selling one template for everything.

## What a promo website is and why business needs it

A promo website is a bold temporary web project for a specific campaign, product launch or event, richer than a landing thanks to animation, interactivity and storytelling, and its goal is a wow effect and audience engagement. Unlike a permanent catalog or a corporate site, it tells the story of a single product or campaign more broadly and more emotionally than an ordinary landing page. The purpose of such a site is not to collect the maximum of requests head-on, but to bring a new brand or product to market, create an impression and draw the visitor into the story. That is exactly why a promo website is ordered for launches, sales and events where creativity and reach matter.

## How a promo website differs from a landing page

A promo website and a landing page are often confused, but their tasks differ. A landing page is a permanent one-pager for a single goal, conversion into a request or a signup, with minimal navigation and everything subordinated to one action. A promo website is built for image, value explanation and engagement, it is larger in content and scenario, with section navigation, a product story, media and animation.

| Parameter | Promo website | Landing page |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Image, wow effect, engagement | One conversion into a request |
| Lifespan | Temporary, for the campaign run | Permanent, works for years |
| Structure | Sections, scenario, navigation | One screen flow for one offer |
| Animation and interactivity | At the core of the idea | Minimal, so it does not disturb the form |
| Target action | Walk through the story, engage | Leave a request |

If the task is only to collect leads for one offer, a [landing page](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/lending/) suits you better, it is cheaper and tuned for conversion. If you need a bold project for a campaign, you are on the right page.

## When you need a promo website

A promo website is justified where the task has a deadline and a creative message. The main occasions:

- **Launch of a new product or brand** to show value and create a first impression.
- **Ad campaign, sale or special offer** to gather attention around the offer for the campaign run.
- **Event, contest or occasion** an event page with participation mechanics and engagement.
- **Brand anniversary and image campaigns** to tell the company story emotionally.

What all these tasks share is being temporary and betting on emotion. The success of a promo website is measured by engagement and reach, not only by the number of leads.

## What promo website development includes

A promo website is made richer than a landing by a set of techniques that hold attention and turn a view into an impression.

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/promo-sait-1.svg | What a promo website includes: animation, interactivity, storytelling, microinteractions, 3D/WebGL, parallax]

- **Animation** holds attention and makes the brand feel alive.
- **Interactivity** engages through quizzes, scroll effects, configurators and calculators.
- **Storytelling** leads the user through the product story from screen to screen.
- **Microinteractions** respond to every move and make the site feel responsive.
- **3D and WebGL** deliver a wow effect and show the product in volume right in the browser.
- **Parallax** creates depth through layered scrolling.

We pick the set for the idea and budget: somewhere neat animation is enough, somewhere a special project with unique mechanics is needed.

## FAQ

**How does a promo website differ from a landing page?**
A landing page is a permanent one-pager for one conversion and one call to action, with minimal navigation. A promo website is a temporary image project for a campaign, richer in content, animation and scenario, and its task is to engage, not just to collect a request. If you need a permanent landing for one offer, see the [landing page](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/lending/).

**What does a promo website mean in simple terms?**
It is a temporary site for a specific campaign, product launch or event. It lives for the campaign run, tells the product story vividly and emotionally, and engages the visitor through animation and interactivity, rather than just showing a price list and a form.

**What tasks is a promo website needed for?**
For launching a new product or brand, for an ad campaign and sale, for an event, contest or brand anniversary. That is, for temporary tasks with a bold creative message, where impression and reach matter.

**How much does it cost to develop a promo website?**
A simple promo website costs [from 40,000 to 80,000 RUB](https://rashdigital.ru/), a site with animation and interactivity [110,000 – 300,000 RUB](https://workspace.ru/), and a complex special project with 3D/WebGL [from 300,000 to 700,000 RUB and above](https://theideal.ru/). The total depends on the complexity of the animations and the uniqueness of the mechanics, and we name the exact estimate after a brief.

**How long does promo website development take?**
A simple promo on a site builder from 1-2 weeks, a site with custom animation 2-6 weeks, a special project with 3D/WebGL from 4-6 weeks and longer. The timeline depends on the amount of animation and integrations.

**Can you make the promo website in time for the campaign start?**
Yes, for campaigns with a fixed date we work backward from the deadline and plan the stages around the campaign start. If time is short, we will propose a format that is realistic to ship on time and say in advance how much animation we can finish.

**What animations and interactivity can be added to a promo website?**
Scroll animations, parallax, microinteractions, quizzes, calculators, configurators and storytelling with a product story walkthrough. We pick the set for the idea and budget, so the effects work for engagement rather than slowing the site down.

**Does a promo website need 3D or WebGL and when is it justified?**
3D and WebGL are justified when you need to show the product in volume or create a strong wow effect, for example for a premium launch. For a simple campaign they are excessive and make the project more expensive, so we propose them only where they truly strengthen the idea.

**Which platform should a promo website be built on, Tilda or coding from scratch?**
A fast promo to a deadline fits [Tilda or a site builder](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/sait-na-tilde/). Complex animation, non-standard UX and 3D call for coding from scratch with JS and GSAP. We choose the platform for the task and the deadline.

**What happens to the promo website after the campaign ends?**
A promo website is temporary, so after the campaign it can be taken down, kept as a brand archive or used to redirect traffic to the main site. We agree the scenario in advance so the domain and analytics do not go to waste.

**Can you collect requests and promo codes on a promo website?**
Yes, we connect a request form, promo code issuing and CRM integration without a problem. But if your task is only lead collection for one offer, it is cheaper and more effective to build a [landing page](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/lending/), and to use a promo website where image and engagement matter.

**How does a promo website differ from a corporate site?**
A promo website is temporary and dedicated to one campaign, product or event, and it lives for the campaign run. A [corporate site](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/korporativnyi-sait/) is permanent and tells about the company as a whole: services, team, contacts. These are different tasks, and they should not be confused.
