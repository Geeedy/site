# Website Support & Maintenance on a Subscription

A website is not a one-time purchase but a working tool that needs constant attention. The engine and plugins get updated, vulnerabilities appear, hosting goes down, the request form breaks, minor edits are needed. We keep your site alive and secure under contract: updates, daily backups, protection, monitoring and minor improvements for a fixed monthly fee. This is a recurring maintenance service, not a one-off job. It is built into our approach to [website development](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/): do it well and then not abandon it.

## What website support is and why you need it

Website support is the regular maintenance of a live site under contract: CMS and plugin updates, backups, protection against hacking, availability monitoring and minor edits for a fixed subscription fee. Unlike a one-off improvement, support runs continuously and prevents problems instead of fighting fires after the fact.

Without maintenance a site degrades unnoticed. An outdated engine becomes the main attack vector, because known vulnerabilities have long been documented and are scanned automatically by bots. A broken form or a failed payment steals requests for weeks until someone notices by chance. A site that has crashed with no fresh backup has to be rebuilt from scratch. Regular support removes these risks and turns website spending from unpredictable emergencies into a clear budget line.

The subscription covers the entire routine loop of site maintenance. A typical scope of work looks like this.

- **Consulting.** Helping site administrators with day-to-day questions.

## Security and backups

Two things save a site in 90% of incidents: a fresh backup and vulnerabilities closed on time. We make regular backups of files and the database and store them separately, so we can restore the site even after a complete hosting failure or a hack. A copy is not an archive for the sake of an archive but a working rollback tool: if an update or an edit broke something, we return the previous state within minutes.

Protection is built on prevention. Timely installation of CMS and plugin updates reduces the risk of exploiting known vulnerabilities, since it is exactly the outdated engine that remains the main attack vector. On top of this monitoring runs: we track suspicious activity and respond to incidents under the SLA. If the site is affected anyway, we restore it from a backup and close the hole the attack came through.

## Minor improvements and site growth

A site lives and changes: update prices, swap a banner, add a section, fix text, insert a new block on a landing page. Such tasks are covered by the subscription within the included hours. We keep transparent records: for every task we log the time spent and show the remaining hours in the monthly report. You always see what you pay for and do not pay for nothing.

Hours are not only routine but also a growth point. As part of maintenance we improve the site for business goals and for search engine requirements. If edits for search optimization have piled up, it makes sense to first check priorities through an [SEO audit](/uslugi/seo-prodvizhenie/seo-audit/) and then roll them out as a batch within support. This way site growth is systematic rather than a chaos of one-off orders.

## How the subscription differs from one-off services

Support is often confused with other website work, though the intent behind them differs. A subscription is the regular maintenance of an existing site, while a redesign, migration and development of a new site are one-off projects with a fixed result and deadline.

| Parameter | Subscription support | Redesign | Migration | New site |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | Regular, every month | One-off | One-off | One-off |
| What happens | The site stays and is maintained | Look and structure change | Hosting or engine changes | Built from scratch |
| Payment | Monthly subscription | Project estimate | Project estimate | Project estimate |
| Result | Site works and is protected | Updated design | Site on a new environment | Finished site |

If you need not to maintain but to refresh the site's look, that is a [website redesign](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/redizayn-sayta/), a separate one-off service. Support keeps the site and holds it in shape, a redesign changes how it looks and is arranged.

## How we work: requests, hours, reports

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/podderzhka-sayta-2.svg | How a maintenance month goes: request → priority → work within hours → report]

Transparency is the basis of trust in support, so the process is set up simply and clearly.

1. **Request.** You submit a task or a failure to the agreed channel. Monitoring can open a request itself if it sees an outage.
2. **Priority.** We assign priority under the SLA. A critical failure jumps the queue with a response from 1 hour, an ordinary edit is put into the plan.
3. **Work.** We complete the task and log the time spent.
4. **Report.** At the end of the month we send a report: what was done, how many hours were used, how many are left.

Unused hours and the rules for accounting for them we fix in the contract in advance, so there are no disputed situations. At any moment you see what the subscription was spent on.

## FAQ

**How does subscription support differ from one-off improvements?**
A subscription is regular maintenance under contract for a fixed fee with a guaranteed response time under the SLA. A one-off improvement is done after the fact, when something has already broken, and each time it is estimated and paid for separately. Support prevents problems, one-off work fixes them after the fact.

**What does the maintenance subscription cover?**
CMS and plugin updates with a compatibility check, regular backups of files and the database, protection against hacking and incident response, monitoring of site availability and critical services, minor improvements within hours and administrator consulting.

**How much does website support cost per month?**
Across the Russian market in 2026 a business card and landing page are maintained in a range of [3,000 – 12,000 RUB/mo, a corporate site 8,000 – 25,000 RUB/mo, an online store 20,000 – 70,000 RUB/mo](https://devorra.ru/blog/tehnicheskaya-podderzhka-sajtov/). The exact price depends on the hour package, stack complexity and support mode. We match the plan to your site's real load.

**What are SLA and response time in support?**
An SLA is a service level agreement. It fixes the scope of work, support hours, request channels, incident priorities and response time. Response time is the time within which the request is taken into work, not the time for a full fix. Resolution depends on the complexity of the task and is agreed separately.

**How fast do you respond if the site goes down?**
A site outage, a payment or form failure is a critical priority. By market norms the response to such incidents is [15 – 30 minutes](https://awg.ru/company/blog/sla/); under our subscription we take a critical failure into work from 1 hour during business hours. Availability monitoring helps us learn about an outage before you do.

**How are work hours tracked and deducted? Do unused ones expire?**
For every task we log the time spent and show the remainder in the monthly report. The rules for carrying over unused hours are written into the contract in advance, so no disputes arise. You always see what the subscription went on.

**Do you make backups and how often? Can the site be restored after a failure?**
Yes. We make regular backups of files and the database and store them separately from the site. On a failure, a bad update or a hack we restore the site from a copy and remove the cause of the incident.

**Do you support a site you did not build?**
Yes. Before taking on someone else's site for maintenance we run a short technical review: we look at the engine, code, access and the project's state. After that we fix the scope of work in the contract and start maintenance.

**Which CMS do you work with?**
1C-Bitrix, WordPress, Tilda, as well as custom-built sites on a proprietary CMS or framework. If you have a non-standard stack, we will discuss it in a consultation before the start.

**Is support the same as a redesign or rebuild of the site?**
No. Support is regular maintenance of the site without changing its look. Updating the design and structure is a separate one-off [website redesign](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/redizayn-sayta/) service. Within support we make minor edits but do not rebuild the site entirely.

**What if I need to move the site to another host or engine?**
Moving a site is a separate one-off migration service, not part of the subscription. We can carry out the move under a separate estimate and then take the new site into regular maintenance after the move.

**Do I need a contract and what does it fix?**
Yes, support runs under contract. It fixes the scope of work, the volume of included hours, an SLA with priorities and response time, request channels, the rules for accounting for hours and the reporting procedure. The contract protects both sides and makes maintenance transparent.
