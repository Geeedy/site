# Beauty Salon CRM: We Select and Configure the System for Your Beauty Business

Appointments get lost in chat, staff either sit idle or work back to back, and the client who came once never returned. That is how a salon runs without a single system of record. A beauty salon CRM brings online booking, the staff schedule and the client base into one place, where every free minute in the chair and every client due for a return visit is visible. We select and configure such a system for a specific salon honestly: in one case it is an industry booking service, in another a universal CRM, and often a combination of the two. This is part of our approach to [CRM implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/): we count the process before and after, rather than selling a box.

## Why a beauty salon needs CRM and online booking

A beauty salon CRM is a system that runs online booking, the schedule of staff and rooms, a card for every client with visit history, and reminds about the appointment automatically. Put simply, instead of a paper journal and the receptionist's notes, one base appears that shows who is booked with whom and when, who has not come in for a while, and how much each specialist earned. The client books at any hour on their own, the receptionist does not call to confirm, and the owner sees chair utilization and revenue in real numbers rather than by feel.

For a small studio or a single specialist this is often enough in a basic form, while a salon chain already calls for tighter automation. If your task is broader than beauty specifics and comes down to the size of the business, look at the separate breakdown of [CRM for small business](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/crm-dlya-malogo-biznesa/), where the segmentation goes by scale rather than by industry.

## What a salon loses without tracking appointments and the client base

While appointments live in the receptionist's head and messenger chats, the losses are invisible, yet they happen every day. A client booked and did not show up, the slot in the schedule stayed empty, the specialist got idle time instead of income. By industry estimates the average no-show rate in beauty is [15–30% of appointments, and in Russia, per NAFI and Profi.ru, it is 18–20%, meaning every sixth client](https://salistica.ru/). Profit lost to such no-shows reaches [up to 22% of a salon's monthly revenue](https://salistica.ru/).

At the same time, online booking is still far from universal: [only about 54% of beauty businesses in Russia](https://salistica.ru/) let the client book on their own, even though demand for self-booking is growing. The [number of online appointments with specialists rose 71% over 2024](https://www.rostovgazeta.ru/), and self-booking is used by [more than 150,000 specialists and salons](https://eanews.ru/). A salon without a system loses twice: on no-shows and on clients who moved to those who are easier to book with.

## What features a beauty salon CRM needs

The beauty specific is that you sell not a product but the specialist's time in the chair. That is why the feature set differs from an ordinary CRM.

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/crm-dlya-salona-krasoty-1.svg | 6 features of a beauty salon CRM]

- **Online booking 24/7.** The client picks the service, specialist and free time themselves, on the website, in social media or via a widget, without calling the receptionist.
- **Schedule of staff and rooms.** Each specialist's timetable, occupancy of chairs and rooms, so there are no clashes or empty slots.
- **Client card and visit history.** What was done, with which specialist, which materials, what preferences and contraindications, when a repeat visit is due.
- **Auto-reminders.** A message in WhatsApp or Telegram a day and an hour before the visit, so the client does not forget and shows up.
- **Loyalty program.** Bonuses, discounts for regulars, packages and promotions that bring the client back instead of letting them go to a competitor.
- **Supplies inventory.** Tracking materials by service, writing off dye and cosmetics, stock control, so the specialist is not left without what they need in the middle of the day.

## Which system to choose: YCLIENTS, DIKIDI or Bitrix24

Here it is important to be honest. For beauty salons in Russia an industry standard has formed: [YCLIENTS, used by about 90% of salon chains](https://toolfox.ru/). We do not pretend to replace it with our own development. Our role is to choose between an industry booking service and a universal CRM and set up what fits your salon specifically, and sometimes to link them together.

## Which integrations we connect for a salon

A booking system is valuable when it is linked with the salon's other tools. We set up integrations for beauty processes.

- **Online cash register per 54-FZ.** The receipt is issued automatically when a service is paid, sales and materials are accounted for by law.
- **Acquiring and online payment.** Prepayment for a visit and selling packages online, which also cuts no-shows.
- **WhatsApp and Telegram.** Auto-reminders, booking confirmations and win-back campaigns in the channels where the client actually reads.
- **Telephony.** A call is tied to the client card, the receptionist sees who is calling and with what history.
- **Booking widget on the website and social media.** A booking button right on the website, in Instagram, VKontakte and on maps, without the client leaving their usual place.

## What we configure during implementation

[IMG: ../assets/images/crm-dlya-salona-krasoty-1.jpg | What we configure when implementing CRM in a beauty salon: base, schedule, reminders]

Implementation is not installing a program, it is moving the salon's work into the system without losing data and without receptionists panicking.

1. **Client base migration.** We pull the history from the old program, Excel or a notebook and move it into client cards with visits and contacts.
2. **Schedule of services and staff.** We set up the price list, procedure durations, each specialist's timetable, rooms and combined services.
3. **Reminder scenarios.** We configure when and in which channel a reminder, confirmation and win-back message go out.
4. **Staff payroll.** We set up calculation by percentages, salaries and services, so payouts are counted from actual visits rather than by hand.
5. **Receptionist training.** We show the team how to manage bookings, work with the client card and not fear the system, so nothing stalls on the very first day.

## FAQ

**Which CRM is best for a beauty salon: YCLIENTS, DIKIDI or Bitrix24?**
There is no single right answer, it all depends on the tasks. YCLIENTS is the industry standard for salons and chains, DIKIDI is good for a start and solo specialists and has a free plan, while Bitrix24 is chosen when you need a full sales pipeline and deals beyond booking. We pick the system in an audit, working from your processes and budget rather than from the brand.

**How does an industry booking service differ from a universal CRM for a salon?**
An industry service like YCLIENTS or DIKIDI is built for beauty from the start: online booking, schedule of staff and rooms, supplies inventory. A universal CRM like Bitrix24 or amoCRM is stronger in the sales pipeline, deals and analytics, and booking in it is configured as a module. Often a combination is optimal: the service handles booking, the CRM handles sales.

**Does a small salon or a single specialist need a CRM?**
Not always in full. A single specialist or a small studio at the start often gets by on the free DIKIDI plan. An integrator is needed when a link with an online cash register, website, messengers and analytics appears, and also when the base and schedule have grown so much that handling them manually is already costly in time and errors.

**How much does turnkey CRM implementation for a salon cost?**
The integrator's work on the market starts at 15,000–30,000 ₽ for online booking with a widget, 40,000–80,000 ₽ for a medium project with the base, cash register and reminders, and 80,000–200,000 ₽ for an extended bundle with telephony and loyalty. On top comes the service subscription. We lock the exact estimate after a short audit of the salon.

**How long does implementation and launching online booking take?**
Basic online booking with a widget launches in 1–2 weeks. A full project with an industry service, CRM, telephony and loyalty program bundle takes from 4 to 8 weeks. The exact term depends on the number of specialists, the size of the old base and the number of integrations.

**How do auto-reminders reduce client no-shows?**
The client gets a message about the appointment a day and an hour before the visit and either shows up or reschedules in advance, freeing the slot. Per industry data, auto-reminders cut no-shows by 35–45%, and clients who booked online themselves miss visits about 20% less often. For a salon this is a direct return of idle specialist time into revenue.

**Can the client base and visit history be migrated from an old program or Excel?**
Yes, this is a standard part of implementation. We export data from the previous system, Excel or even a paper journal and move it into client cards with contacts and visit history. Before loading we clean duplicates and put the base in order, so you start with correct data.

**How do you set up online booking on the website and social media?**
We place a booking widget on your website, add a booking button in social media and on maps, and link them to the staff schedule. The client picks the service, specialist and time in their usual place, and the appointment goes straight into the system without manual transfer by the receptionist.

**Is integration with an online cash register per 54-FZ needed and how does it work?**
If you accept payment for services, a cash register per 54-FZ is mandatory. We link the booking system with the online cash register so that when a visit is paid the receipt is issued automatically, and sales and material write-offs are accounted for correctly. This removes manual work from the receptionist and covers the legal requirements.

**How does the CRM calculate staff payroll and schedule load?**
The system counts payouts from actual visits by set rules: percentages of services, salaries, bonuses for materials or sales. The owner sees each specialist's schedule load, idle time and output. This removes manual payroll counting at month end and disputes over who did how much.

**Will you set up supplies inventory and material tracking by service?**
Yes. We enter the supplies catalog, tie material write-offs to services and configure stock control. The system shows what is running out and when, so the specialist is not left without dye or cosmetics in the middle of the day, and purchases follow actual consumption rather than guesswork.

**We already work in YCLIENTS, what can you add?**
YCLIENTS is the industry standard, and there is no point replacing it. We strengthen what surrounds it: we connect telephony, messengers for win-back campaigns, acquiring, link it with a universal CRM for the sales pipeline and analytics, and set up end-to-end reporting. In other words, we build out the bundle so that booking data works toward revenue and retention.
