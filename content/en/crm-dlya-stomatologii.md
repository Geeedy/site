# Dental CRM: Tooth Chart, Scheduling and Patient Recalls

In most dental practices, appointments live in a paper logbook, the treatment plan sits in the doctor's head, and patients disappear after the first visit. Chairs sit idle, no one reminds patients about hygiene, and the clinic pays for new patients instead of bringing back its own. A dental CRM keeps an electronic patient record with a tooth chart and treatment plan, shows chair and doctor load, and reminds patients about recalls on its own. We are an independent integrator: we pick a system to fit your clinic format and configure it around real dental workflows rather than selling a boxed product. This is part of our [CRM implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/) service, where the effect is measured before and after.

## Who a dental CRM fits

A dental CRM is a system that keeps an electronic patient record with a tooth chart and treatment plan, chair and doctor scheduling, reminders about recalls and hygiene, and handling of the price list and insurance. Unlike a generic CRM with no medical specifics, an industry solution stores the dental formula, tooth statuses and a staged treatment plan right in the patient card ([dental-cloud.ru](https://dental-cloud.ru/), [infodent.ru](https://infodent.ru/)). It fits a private office with one or two chairs, a standalone dental clinic, and a small chain of locations.

By specialty, the solution equally covers therapy, prosthetics, implantology and orthodontics: each needs the dental formula, a staged treatment plan, and control over the patient returning for the next stage. If you run a multi-specialty medical clinic rather than dentistry alone, see [CRM for a medical clinic](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/dlya-kliniki/): there segmentation follows general medicine, while here the focus is strictly on dental specifics.

## What a CRM solves in dentistry

Without a system, a dental practice loses money quietly, because the losses do not show up in the revenue report. Forgotten recalls, empty chairs, and a treatment plan kept in one doctor's head appear on no list. Acquiring a new patient costs several times more than retaining a familiar one, so systematic work with the base and with recalls becomes a key source of clinic growth ([vc.ru](https://vc.ru/), [retailcrm.ru](https://www.retailcrm.ru/)). A dental CRM closes these gaps with a set of tools:

- **Tooth chart and treatment plan** - the dental formula with statuses and a staged plan with pricing right in the patient card.
- **Scheduling and chair load** - a schedule by chair and doctor, online booking and idle-time control.
- **Recall and hygiene reminders** - triggers for hygiene every 6 months and for the next treatment stage.
- **Price list, insurance and private health insurance (DMS)** - a price list with stages, plan cost calculation, invoices and work with insurers.
- **Patient base** - a single history of visits, images and payments instead of a paper logbook and spreadsheets.
- **Integrations** - links with the medical information system (MIS), X-ray, telephony and messengers.

It is exactly the combination "tooth chart and treatment plan + chair schedule + return reminders" that sets a dental CRM apart from a plain contact list.

## Tooth chart and treatment plan in the patient card

[IMG: ../assets/images/crm-dlya-stomatologii-1.jpg | Tooth chart and treatment plan in the patient card in a dental CRM]

The core of the industry solution is the electronic patient record built around the dental formula. The tooth chart records the status of each tooth: healthy, caries, filling, crown, implant, extracted. The doctor marks changes right on the diagram, and the picture of the whole mouth comes together in one place ([dental-cloud.ru](https://dental-cloud.ru/), [klientiks.ru](https://klientiks.ru/)).

The treatment plan grows out of the tooth chart. It breaks down into stages with specific procedures, timelines and pricing, and the stages roll up into the patient's overall invoice ([dental-cloud.ru](https://dental-cloud.ru/), [klientiks.ru](https://klientiks.ru/)). The doctor and the administrator see which stage is done and which is waiting to be booked, so the patient does not drop out of treatment halfway. Alongside sits the history of visits and images, so there is no need to dig up papers or re-question the patient before the next visit. The card thus becomes a single source of truth about the person rather than a set of scattered notes.

## Chair and doctor scheduling and load

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/crm-dlya-stomatologii-1.svg | Dental patient journey in CRM: online booking, visit and tooth chart, treatment plan, recall reminder, hygiene after 6 months, repeat sales]

Scheduling in dentistry revolves not only around doctors but also around chairs. The CRM shows the load of each room and chair, procedure durations and idle time, so the administrator sees where there is a slot and where the load is already tight. Online booking and chair occupancy control raise room utilization and clinic revenue ([klientiks.ru](https://klientiks.ru/), [infoclinica.ru](https://infoclinica.ru/)).

The system helps distribute the load across doctors and fit slot length to the procedure type, so an implant and a routine cleaning do not take the same time in the grid. Online booking from the website and aggregators feeds into the shared schedule rather than living separately. If you want to fully automate booking in a messenger, that is handled by a [chatbot for patient booking](/uslugi/chat-boty/dlya-zapisi/): it takes over time selection and confirmation, and the slot drops straight into the chair schedule.

## Recall and hygiene reminders: reducing churn

The main source of growth for a dental practice is the return of a familiar patient, not just a new flow. The CRM runs trigger reminders that bring the person back for a scheduled visit and hygiene via SMS and WhatsApp ([dental-cloud.ru](https://dental-cloud.ru/), [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/)). The set of triggers is usually as follows:

- **Hygiene every 6 months** - a reminder to book a scheduled cleaning.
- **Recall by treatment plan** - a nudge to return for the next stage already in the card.
- **Reactivation of "dormant" patients** - a touch to those who have not come in a while, with an incentive to book.
- **Booking confirmation** - a reminder of the date and time to cut no-show rates.
- **Post-visit review** - a feedback request that also brings the patient back into dialogue.

Since retention costs less than acquisition, systematic reminders reduce churn and bring patients back for a recall instead of the clinic paying for a new one each time ([vc.ru](https://vc.ru/), [retailcrm.ru](https://www.retailcrm.ru/)).

## Price list, insurance and private health insurance (DMS)

A dental CRM works not only with treatment but also with the money around it. A price list with treatment stages, automatic plan cost calculation, generation of invoices and acts, and accounting of payments under private health insurance (DMS) and insurance programs come together in one loop ([klientiks.ru](https://klientiks.ru/), [infodent.ru](https://infodent.ru/)). Below is exactly what we configure in this block.

| What we configure | How it works | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Price list with stages | Each procedure in the treatment plan is tied to a price from the list | The plan cost is calculated automatically, without a manual calculator |
| Plan cost calculation | Treatment stages sum into the patient's overall invoice | The patient sees a clear estimate, the administrator does not miscount |
| Invoices and acts | Documents are generated from the card based on actual visits | Less manual work and fewer discrepancies in reporting |
| Private health insurance (DMS) and insurers | Accounting of payments under DMS and insurance programs | Transparent settlements with insurers, nothing gets lost |

Thus the financial part stops living in a separate spreadsheet and ties into the treatment plan and tooth chart of the specific patient.

## Integrations: MIS, telephony, messengers

[IMG: ../assets/images/crm-dlya-stomatologii-2.jpg | Integration of a dental CRM with MIS, telephony and messengers at the clinic reception]

The value of the solution unfolds in links with the clinic's other systems. The main loop is the medical information system (MIS) and X-ray or radiovisiograph: images and medical data feed into the patient card, and the tooth chart and medical record are kept by the industry MIS itself ([infoclinica.ru](https://infoclinica.ru/), [dental-cloud.ru](https://dental-cloud.ru/)). The second loop is telephony: IP telephony with call recording ties calls to the card, so the administrator sees the patient's contact history.

The third loop is communications: WhatsApp and SMS notifications, online booking from the website and aggregators all converge into one window. If you use amoCRM or Bitrix24 for the client funnel and requests, they cover calls and messengers, while the tooth chart and medical record stay with the MIS; the integration links the CRM funnel with the medical system ([amocrm.ru](https://www.amocrm.ru/), [klientiks.ru](https://klientiks.ru/)). If you need broader [AI clinic process automation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-ii/avtomatizaciya-kliniki/), that is a separate service: here we are responsible strictly for the CRM built for dentistry.

## FAQ

**How does a dental CRM differ from a generic CRM and from a MIS?**
A generic CRM runs the client and funnel without medical specifics. An industry dental CRM adds a tooth chart, a treatment plan and chair scheduling, while a MIS stores the medical record and images. In practice they are linked: the funnel and communications in the CRM, the dental formula and medical data in the MIS.

**How are a patient's tooth chart and treatment plan kept in the CRM?**
The tooth chart records the status of each tooth: healthy, caries, filling, crown, implant, extracted. The treatment plan breaks down into stages with procedures, timelines and pricing, and the stages roll up into the patient's overall invoice. All of this is stored in the card along with the history of visits and images.

**How do you set up chair and doctor scheduling and remove idle time?**
The schedule is built by chair and doctor at once: the system shows room load, procedure durations and idle time. Online booking and chair occupancy control raise utilization, so the administrator sees free slots and distributes the load without empty gaps in the grid.

**How does the CRM remind patients about recalls and hygiene?**
The system runs triggers: hygiene every 6 months, the next stage of the treatment plan, reactivation of "dormant" patients, booking confirmation. Reminders go out via SMS and WhatsApp automatically, so the patient returns for a scheduled visit and the administrator does not need to call the base by hand.

**How much does the CRM reduce churn and no-shows?**
The exact figure depends on the clinic, but the principle is straightforward: booking confirmations cut no-shows, and recall reminders bring patients back for the next stage. Since retention costs less than acquiring a new patient, systematic work with the base noticeably reduces churn.

**Can you handle the price list, invoices and work with insurance and private health insurance (DMS)?**
Yes. We configure a price list with treatment stages, automatic plan cost calculation, generation of invoices and acts, and accounting of payments under DMS and insurance programs. The financial part ties into the specific patient's treatment plan rather than living in a separate spreadsheet.

**Which CRM to choose for dentistry - Dental Cloud, Infodent, Klientiks or amoCRM with a MIS?**
It depends on the tasks. Dental Cloud, Infodent, Klientiks and Infoclinica are industry solutions with a tooth chart and medical record. amoCRM or Bitrix24 cover the funnel and communications, while the tooth chart is kept by the MIS through integration. We pick the specific combination at the audit for your clinic.

**Can the CRM be linked with a MIS, X-ray and telephony?**
Yes. The industry system links with a MIS and X-ray or radiovisiograph, IP telephony with call recording, and messengers. Images feed into the card, calls are tied to the patient, and notifications go out to WhatsApp and SMS from one window.

**How much does turnkey dental CRM implementation cost?**
The estimate is made up of the CRM or MIS license, the implementation work, and integrations with MIS, X-ray and telephony. The total depends on the number of chairs and doctors, the set of specialties and the state of the data. We lock the exact figure after a short audit for your clinic.

**How long do implementation and staff training take?**
A basic project for one office takes weeks: audit, tooth chart and price list setup, base migration, training and launch. For a clinic or chain the timeline grows with the number of integrations and locations, but the principle is the same - we launch in stages so appointments do not stop.

**Can the patient base be migrated from a logbook, Excel or an old system?**
Yes. Migrating and consolidating the base from a paper logbook, spreadsheets or a prior system is a standard part of implementation. We clean duplicates and match cards so the history of visits, treatment plan and patient contacts are not lost in the transition.

**Will the solution fit a single office or only a chain of clinics?**
It fits a single office, a clinic, and a small chain alike. For an office a simpler configuration is used, and for a chain we add access rights and consolidated reports across locations. We scale the tooth chart, chair and reminder setup to your size.
