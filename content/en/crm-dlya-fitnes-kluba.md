## Why a Fitness Club Needs a CRM and Tracking System

CRM for a fitness club is a system that keeps the client base together with memberships, the class schedule and attendance control in one place. It shows whose membership is ending or frozen, who has not come in for a while and who is due for a win-back, and how loaded the gyms and trainers are by the hour. A notebook or a spreadsheet only records the fact of a visit, while CRM turns the base into a managed flow of renewals: it reminds the client that a membership is ending, runs a pipeline for ad leads and calculates load. It suits a club or studio of any size, and if the question is really about business scale rather than the industry, see our breakdown of [CRM for small business](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/crm-dlya-malogo-biznesa/).

## What a Club Loses Without Membership and Attendance Tracking

The value of a system is clearest where the absence of tracking directly costs money. Without CRM a club loses on four fronts at once.

- **Missed renewals.** The club does not see whose membership is ending and fails to offer a renewal in time. Retaining a current client is cheaper than acquiring a new one, so a missed renewal is lost revenue with almost no cost to recover it.
- **Lost ad leads.** Requests from paid search, social media and the website settle in different messengers, some go unanswered, and without a pipeline you cannot tell which channel actually brings clients.
- **Idle gyms.** Group and daytime slots sit empty because nobody counts load by the hour or pulls clients into under-filled sessions.
- **Unsold freezes and manual chaos.** The front desk keeps terms, visit limits and freezes in their head and in spreadsheets, which leads to calculation errors and disputes with clients.

## What Functions a CRM for a Fitness Club Needs

The industry specifics are that a club sells not a product but a term and visits. So the feature set differs from an ordinary CRM.

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/crm-dlya-fitnes-kluba-1.svg | 6 CRM functions for a fitness club: memberships, schedule, attendance, renewals, trainers, loyalty]

- **Membership and freeze tracking** - membership types, terms, visit limits, rules and freeze tracking.
- **Group and personal schedule** - a grid of group classes and booking for personal training with gym and trainer load.
- **Attendance control** - visit check-in, including automatically via turnstiles and electronic access cards.
- **Retention and renewals** - visibility of ending memberships and auto-reminders about renewal.
- **Trainer and payroll tracking** - trainer load by schedule and payroll calculation for sessions delivered.
- **Loyalty programs** - bonuses, discounts and promotions that bring the client back and increase visit frequency.

## Which Integrations We Connect for a Club

A standalone system solves half the task. The benefit comes from a bundle in which the membership, payment, gym entry and reminder work as one process.

- **Online cash register per 54-FZ** - receipts for the sale and renewal of memberships are issued automatically.
- **Turnstiles and electronic access cards** - gym entry marks the visit and checks for a valid membership.
- **Acquiring** - card payment at the front desk and online payment of renewals.
- **Mobile app with booking** - the client books classes themselves and sees the membership balance.
- **WhatsApp and Telegram reminders** - auto-messages about classes, renewals and freezes.
- **Telephony** - calls are tied to the client card, nothing gets lost.
- **Ad leads** - requests from the website, paid search and social media drop into the pipeline automatically.

## FAQ

**Which CRM is better for a fitness club: Mobifitness, 1C:Fitness Club or YCLIENTS?**
There is no universally "best" one, the choice depends on the club. Mobifitness is convenient with its bundle of tracking and an app, 1C:Fitness Club is strong in deep tracking, finance and chain management, YCLIENTS is good at online booking but weaker in heavy industry tracking of memberships and payroll. We select the system for your processes during the audit.

**How does an industry fitness system differ from a universal CRM like Bitrix24 or amoCRM?**
An industry system handles memberships, freezes, gym schedules, access control and trainer payroll out of the box. A universal CRM covers the pipeline of ad leads and end-to-end analytics, but it has no industry tracking. They are often connected together: [Bitrix24](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/bitrix24/) or [amoCRM](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/amocrm/) for leads plus an industry system for club tracking.

**Does a small studio or a single trainer need a CRM?**
Often a small studio will do with basic industry SaaS from [1,760 RUB/mo](https://spark.ru) without complex integrations. An integrator is needed when a bundle with a 54-FZ cash register, turnstiles, an app and ad leads appears. We will honestly say which case is yours and will not push extras.

**How do memberships and freezes work in a CRM?**
The system stores membership types, validity terms, visit limits and freeze rules. Each visit deducts a visit, a freeze pauses the term by the set rules, and the system shows ending memberships in advance for renewal. This removes manual tracking and disputes with clients.

**How much does turnkey CRM implementation for a fitness club cost?**
It is made up of the system subscription, usually [from 1,760 to 2,990 RUB/mo](https://spark.ru), and one-time integrator services. Implementation with access control costs [from 55,000 RUB per entry point](https://fitness365.ru), and custom development for a club is [350,000–500,000 RUB](https://falcon.web-punk.ru). We calculate the exact quote after the audit.

**How long does implementation and launch of the system take?**
Basic implementation of industry SaaS with data migration and training takes [from 1 to 3 weeks](https://fitbase.ru) with annual payment. A full project with a bundle of the system, cash register, turnstiles, app and lead pipeline fits into 6–12 weeks.

**How do you set up access control via turnstiles and electronic cards?**
The turnstile or entry point is integrated with the system: a card or the client app opens the entry, and at that same moment the system marks the visit and checks for a valid membership. Implementation with equipment integration runs [from 55,000 RUB per one entry point](https://fitness365.ru).

**How do auto-reminders help renew memberships and reduce churn?**
The system sees ending memberships in advance and sends the client a reminder about renewal in WhatsApp or Telegram, and it offers a return to those who have not come in for a while. The club stops losing clients to a silent term ending and increases the share of renewals.

**Can the client base and membership history be migrated from old software or Excel?**
Yes. Migrating the base and membership history is a standard part of implementation. Many industry systems do free data migration with annual payment, and we make sure membership types, terms and remaining visits transfer correctly.

**Is integration with an online cash register per 54-FZ needed and how does it work?**
Yes, if the club takes payment directly. The cash register is integrated with the system, and on the sale or renewal of a membership a receipt is issued automatically per 54-FZ requirements. This removes manual duplication of sales and eliminates the risk of errors in fiscal reporting.

**How does the system calculate trainer payroll and gym load by schedule?**
The system keeps the schedule of group and personal sessions, records sessions delivered and calculates trainer payroll from them by your rules. Gym load is visible by the hour, which helps pull clients into empty slots and plan the grid.

**We already work in Mobifitness or 1C:Fitness, what can you add?**
If an industry system is already in place, we strengthen it with the bundle it usually lacks: the pipeline of ad leads in a universal CRM, end-to-end analytics by channel, integration of the cash register, turnstiles and app, auto-scenarios for renewals. That is, we build out the missing nodes rather than replacing the working tracking.
