## Why businesses choose a 1C-Bitrix website

A 1C-Bitrix site is a site on the commercial "1C-Bitrix: Site Management" CMS, a system with a legal Russian license, ready-made 1C integration and built-in speed and protection mechanisms. Businesses choose it when a project outgrows a website builder and needs complex logic. Per market data, 1C-Bitrix leads among online-store CMS platforms in Russia with a share of about [60.8%](https://www.sostav.ru/publication/rejting-cms-2025-60821.html) in the e-commerce segment.

Three reasons businesses settle on this engine:

- **1C exchange out of the box.** Products, prices, stock and orders sync with "1C:Enterprise" over a standard protocol, without homegrown workarounds.
- **Russian license and support.** A legal product with vendor updates and technical support, which matters for companies with import-independence requirements.
- **Room to grow.** One engine handles a corporate site, a large catalog and an internal portal alike, so the project does not need a rewrite as it scales.

## 1C-Bitrix (site CMS) and Bitrix24 (CRM) are different products

This is the most common mix-up, and it is worth clearing up before the start. There is one brand but two products, and they solve different tasks. Confusing them is costly: a site and a CRM need different licenses, teams and timelines.

| Parameter | 1C-Bitrix: Site Management | Bitrix24 |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | External site, catalog, portal | CRM and company internal portal |
| For whom | Visitors and clients online | Staff, sales department, managers |
| What it does | Shows content and products, takes requests | Runs deals, tasks, the sales funnel |
| License | One-time purchase of a CMS edition | Bitrix24 subscription or box |
| Project type | Website development | CRM implementation |

A simple rule: everything your client sees in a browser is a 1C-Bitrix site. Everything your managers work in inside the company is Bitrix24. We handle the setup and implementation of the second product separately on the [Bitrix24 implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/bitrix24/) page, and this page is only about the site.

## Integrating the site with 1C:Enterprise

The main reason to choose this engine is seamless exchange with the accounting system. The site and "1C:Enterprise" exchange data over the CommerceML standard in XML format through a native exchange endpoint. The catalog, prices and stock flow from 1C to the site, while placed orders go from the site back to 1C. The exchange is two-way and runs on a schedule, so prices and availability on the site stay current without manual entry.

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/sait-na-bitrikse-1.svg | Diagram of a 1C-Bitrix site exchanging with 1C:Enterprise via CommerceML]

This removes double work and manual-transfer errors: the manager keeps the product list in the familiar 1C, and the site picks up changes on its own. It is precisely for this integration that the Bitrix engine is most often chosen over lighter platforms.

## SEO, speed and security on Bitrix

The engine covers the technical basics for search and load without third-party plugins. For SEO out of the box you get human-readable URLs for all content types, control of title and description at the section and element level, auto-generation of sitemap.xml and robots.txt, canonical and redirect setup.

Speed is handled by composite mode: the engine serves the page as ready static HTML from cache in fractions of a second, while dynamic blocks such as the cart and personal account load with a separate request. A fast page response is both a ranking factor and a convenience for the visitor.

Security on the commercial editions is covered by proactive protection, a built-in web firewall. It filters SQL injection and cross-site scripting, guards against password brute-forcing and keeps an attack log. For a site tied to an accounting system and orders, this lowers the risk of leaks and downtime.

## FAQ

**How is a 1C-Bitrix site different from Bitrix24?**
These are different products of one ecosystem. 1C-Bitrix: Site Management is a CMS for the external site that your clients see online. Bitrix24 is a CRM and internal portal where your staff work and deals are run. On this page we build the site. If you need a CRM, see [Bitrix24 implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/bitrix24/).

**Which sites is 1C-Bitrix right for, and for which is it overkill?**
The engine is justified when you need a complex catalog, 1C exchange, high load or the prospect of growing into a portal. For a simple landing page or business card without integrations it is overkill, and it makes more sense to take a lighter platform like [Tilda](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/sait-na-tilde/).

**Do you need a 1C-Bitrix license and how much does it cost?**
Yes, the engine is paid; the license is bought once. Editions for 2026 cost from 7,100 RUB for Start to 96,500 RUB for Business, and there is Enterprise for a portal. We issue the license to your company so the rights belong to you.

**Which edition should I choose for my site?**
For a corporate site Standard is usually enough, for a site with a catalog and 1C exchange you need Small Business, and for a large store or high load teams take Business. We select the edition at the brief so you do not overpay for capabilities you will not use.

**How is exchange between the site and 1C:Enterprise set up?**
The exchange runs over the CommerceML standard in XML format through a native exchange endpoint. Products, prices and stock are exported from 1C to the site, while orders return from the site to 1C. The exchange is two-way and runs on a schedule, so the data stays current without manual entry.

**Can an existing site be migrated to 1C-Bitrix?**
Yes. We migrate content, structure and the catalog from the old site, keep the page URLs and set up redirects so you do not lose search positions. Before the migration we build a map of the old and new URLs.

**Is a Bitrix site the same as a Bitrix online store?**
Not quite. An online store is a site with a full cart, payment and delivery, and that is a separate service. On this page we build corporate sites, catalogs and portals. We do make a showcase with products and 1C exchange, but a full store is discussed separately.

**Bitrix or Tilda/WordPress: which to choose?**
Bitrix is chosen for a complex catalog, 1C integration and a legal Russian license with support. [Tilda](/uslugi/razrabotka-saitov/sait-na-tilde/) suits landing pages and quick launches, WordPress suits blogs and media without a tight link to 1C. We select the platform to fit the task, not out of habit.

**How much does a turnkey 1C-Bitrix site cost?**
The market benchmarks are as follows: a corporate site 200,000 – 900,000 RUB, a site with a catalog from 400,000 RUB to 1.5 million RUB, a portal 1.5 – 5 million RUB. The sum includes the license, design, development, integrations and support. We lock the exact estimate after the brief.

**How long does development take?**
We build a corporate site in 3 – 4 weeks, a site with a catalog in 6 – 10 weeks, and a large portal from 3 – 4 months. The timeline depends on the catalog size, the number of integrations and the speed of approvals on your side.

**Is a Bitrix site fast, and what about security?**
Speed comes from composite mode: the page is served from cache as static HTML in fractions of a second. Security is covered by proactive protection, a built-in web firewall that filters attacks, guards against password brute-forcing and keeps a log. For a site tied to 1C and orders, this lowers the risks.

**Is post-launch support needed and how much does it cost?**
Support is advisable: the engine and modules get updates, you need backups, 1C exchange monitoring and minor tweaks. We hand over the site with support and agree on the format, from one-off work on request to regular maintenance on a subscription tailored to your scope of tasks.
