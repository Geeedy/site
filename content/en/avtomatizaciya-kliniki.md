## What clinic automation is and why you need it

Clinic automation is the move of a facility's repetitive operational processes (booking, scheduling, reminders, health-record keeping, document flow, medication tracking, and reporting) into digital scenarios that the system runs without manual staff involvement. Unlike installing a single program, this is a bundle of several systems tied into a single loop around your EHR/MIS.

Why a clinic needs it. Manual processes cost working time and money: the administrator calls patients instead of booking new ones, the doctor spends the appointment on paperwork, and a forgotten visit leaves an empty slot that no one fills. Automation lifts this load, frees staff to work with patients, and gives the director a transparent picture of the load. Separately, it lowers the risk of document errors and helps meet the requirements for handling medical data.

We automate the clinic's entire operational loop, not a single task:

1. **Online booking.** The patient books from the website, a messenger, or by phone, and the slot lands in the doctor's schedule at once, with no double bookings.
2. **Doctor scheduling.** A single calendar by room and specialist, accounting for vacations, shifts, and appointment length.
3. **Auto-reminders.** A cascade of SMS and messenger messages before the appointment, an offer to reschedule instead of cancel, and filling of freed slots from the waiting list.
4. **Electronic health records and documents.** EHR keeping, templates for protocols, consents, and contracts, electronic signature, and document exchange between departments.
5. **Medication stock tracking.** Balances, write-offs, expiry dates, and auto-orders for purchasing.
6. **Management reporting.** Doctor and room load, revenue, share of cancellations and no-shows, and bottlenecks on the director's dashboard.

Each of these blocks connects modularly, so you can start with two or three processes and grow the loop as the clinic is ready.

## AI doctor assistant

An AI doctor assistant is a tool that transcribes speech during the appointment, drafts the protocol and conclusion, and suggests the record structure, while the final decision and responsibility stay with the doctor. The assistant saves time on paperwork, it does not make diagnoses: the doctor reviews and approves the text and edits it if needed.

In practice it looks like this: during the appointment the assistant keeps a transcript, after the appointment it hands a ready protocol draft into the EHR, and the doctor only needs to proofread and sign. The document fills in over the course of the consultation instead of being copied into the record from memory in the evening. We split out voice call analytics as a separate service, [speech analytics](/uslugi/vnedrenie-ii/rechevaya-analitika/): there we cover quality control of the call center and front-desk staff, not the medical appointment.

## Reducing patient no-shows and automation effects

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/avtomatizaciya-kliniki-2.svg | Clinic automation effects in numbers]
[IMG: ../assets/images/avtomatizaciya-kliniki-2.jpg | Online booking and doctor scheduling in a clinic system]

A patient no-show is a direct loss: the doctor and room sit idle, and a slot booked in advance can no longer be sold. Automatic reminders fight this head-on. The system sends a cascade of messages a day and a couple of hours before the appointment, offers a reschedule instead of a cancellation, and slots patients from the waiting list into freed windows. International studies of outpatient practice show that automatic SMS reminders [noticeably cut the share of no-shows compared with no reminders](https://www.cochrane.org/CD007458/EPOC_mobile-phone-messaging-reminders-attendance-healthcare-appointments), and this effect reproduces consistently across different clinics.

Beyond cutting no-shows, automation delivers three accompanying effects. First, administrators stop spending hours on manual calls and work with live patients instead. Second, slot occupancy grows because windows are closed from the waiting list automatically. Third, the director sees doctor and room load in real time and reallocates resources before idle time appears. We forecast the concrete numbers for your clinic at the audit stage, based on your current cancellation share and schedule structure.

## The Russian data-localization law, medical data, and personal data protection

Data about a patient's health is a special category of personal data, for which the [Russian data-localization law (152-FZ) "On Personal Data"](http://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&nd=102108261) sets stricter processing rules than for ordinary personal data. Clinic automation touches exactly this data, so we design the legal loop together with the technical one.

What this means in practice. The clinic remains the personal data operator, and we act as the processor under a mandate contract. Processing special-category data requires the patient's informed consent, and the operator itself must be entered into the [register of operators processing personal data maintained by Roskomnadzor](https://pd.rkn.gov.ru/operators-registry/operators-list/). By law, the data of Russian citizens is stored and processed on servers within Russia, so the solution is deployed in your loop or in a certified cloud. We set up access control, channel encryption, action logging, and consent keeping, so that automation creates no risk during regulator inspections.
