# CRM for Auto Service: We Select and Implement a System for Work Orders, Inventory, and Return for Service

An auto service shop loses money not on repairs, but between visits. A request from the website went unanswered, the customer did not come back for scheduled service, a mechanic wrote the work order on paper, and a part slipped past the books. CRM for auto service pulls this operational loop into one system: work orders with statuses, the history of each vehicle by VIN and plate, parts inventory, and service reminders. We are an independent integrator and select a system to fit your shop rather than selling one program. This is part of our approach to [CRM implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/): first we break down the processes of your shop, then we configure what will actually bring customers and money back.

## Why an auto service shop needs a CRM

CRM for auto service is a system that runs not only sales, but the entire operational loop of the shop: work orders with repair statuses, vehicle history by VIN, plate, and mileage, parts inventory, service reminders, and the customer return funnel ([megaplan.ru](https://www.megaplan.ru/blog/crm/), [kaiten.ru](https://kaiten.ru/), 2026). A plain address book or spreadsheet remembers a contact, while an industry CRM remembers the car itself and everything done to it.

It is time to implement one if at least one of these three applies to you:

- requests from the phone, website, and messengers get lost because they never come together in one window;
- you do not know which customers have not come back for service in a long time, and you do not remind them;
- work orders and parts balances live in a notebook or scattered files.

## What a repair shop loses without a CRM

The main leak in an auto service shop is the lost return customer. At [80% of auto service shops the share of repeat visits does not exceed 20–25%](https://bitrix24.team-b.ru/), and with service and seasonal-work reminders set up, the figure climbs to 55–65%. The gap between those numbers is exactly the money the shop leaves on the table.

The math is simple. At an average ticket of [8,000 RUB and 200 active customers, even 30% non-return means minus 480,000 RUB of revenue per year](https://gipix-lite.ru/) on repeat visits alone. That does not include lost requests no one handled and misgrading in the parts inventory. For a small shop this is often a matter of survival, so a CRM pays off faster than it seems, especially if you run a [small business](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/crm-dlya-malogo-biznesa/) with a limited staff.

## What features an auto service CRM needs

Industry logic matters more than a pretty interface. The baseline set, without which a CRM for a repair shop does not work:

[INFOGRAPHIC: ../assets/infographics/crm-dlya-avtoservisa-1.svg | Auto service CRM features: work orders, VIN, inventory, online booking, service reminders]

- **Work orders with statuses.** Intake, diagnostics, repair, handover; every stage is visible and nothing gets lost between bays.
- **Vehicle history by VIN and plate.** Entering [by VIN or plate auto-fills the record](https://www.autodealer.ru/), the data is pulled in automatically, and the whole repair history is tied to the car.
- **Online booking.** A website widget and booking through messengers, so the customer reserves a slot without a call.
- **Parts inventory.** Balances, reservation against a work order, linking items to jobs.
- **Service reminders.** Automatic touches before scheduled maintenance and seasonal tire changes.
- **Return funnel.** The customer does not drop off after a repair, but enters a cycle of repeat visits.

## Specialized or universal CRM for auto service

The fork in the choice runs not between brands, but between two classes of system. Industry CRMs (STOCRM, AutoDealer Online, 1C:Auto Service, Splus) deliver [the work order, labor hours, parts inventory, and mechanic payroll out of the box](https://kaiten.ru/). Universal platforms are strong in the funnel and communications, and add industry logic through integrations and customization.

| Criterion | Industry CRM (STOCRM, AutoDealer) | Universal (Bitrix24, amoCRM) |
|---|---|---|
| Work orders and labor hours | Out of the box | Through setup and customization |
| Parts inventory | Deep industry accounting | Basic, strengthened by integration |
| VIN and vehicle record | Built in | Custom field, auto-fill via integration |
| Sales funnel and communications | Simpler | A strong point |
| Flexibility for non-standard processes | Lower | Higher |

A simple rule: the deeper your inventory and production accounting, the closer you are to an industry system; the more important sales, marketing, and multichannel communications are, the more universal platforms come into play. We break down the specific per-platform setup on separate pages about [Bitrix24 implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/bitrix24/) and [amoCRM implementation](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/amocrm/).

## Integrations for auto service

A CRM without integrations is half the value. For an auto service shop the critical links close the call, the booking, the inventory, and the checkout:

- **IP telephony** for capturing 100% of calls and a customer record by incoming number ([stocrm.ru](https://stocrm.ru/), [bitrix24.ru](https://www.bitrix24.ru/), 2026).
- **Online booking and widget** on the website and in messengers.
- **Inventory and parts catalogs** for connecting suppliers and price lists through ABCP and similar services ([abcp.ru](https://www.abcp.ru/), 2026).
- **SMS and messengers** for service reminders and repair statuses.
- **1C** for exchanging item lists, documents, and balances.
- **Online checkout under 54-FZ** for a receipt straight from the work order.

## FAQ

**How is a CRM for auto service different from a regular CRM?**
A regular CRM runs contacts and deals, while a CRM for auto service runs the car: work orders with repair statuses, history by VIN and plate, parts inventory, and service reminders. It remembers not only the customer, but everything done to their vehicle, and brings them back for scheduled maintenance.

**Does a small shop with 2–3 bays need a CRM?**
Yes, and often it needs one first of all. At a small shop every lost customer is more noticeable, and non-return for service hits revenue harder. For a micro shop, inexpensive cloud systems or a basic setup of a universal platform without extra features will do.

**Can service history be kept by VIN and plate?**
Yes. Entering by VIN or plate auto-fills the vehicle record, the data is pulled in automatically, and the whole history of repairs, replacements, and recommendations is tied to a specific car. On the next visit the mechanic sees what was done before.

**How does a CRM bring customers back for scheduled service?**
The system stores the date and mileage of the last visit and reminds the customer before scheduled service or a seasonal tire change on its own. Service reminders bring [up to 30–35% of customers back for a repeat visit, and upsells raise the average ticket by 20–30%](https://nextgen-dc.ru/).

**Can parts inventory and balances be tracked in a CRM?**
Yes. Industry CRMs track parts inventory, balances, and reservation against a work order out of the box, while universal ones strengthen the inventory through integration with 1C and supplier catalogs. Items are linked to jobs, so parts consumption is visible per repair.

**Specialized CRM or Bitrix24 and amoCRM: which to choose?**
It depends on the size of the shop and the depth of the inventory. The deeper the production accounting and inventory, the closer an industry system (STOCRM, AutoDealer); the more important sales and communications, the stronger the universal platforms. We break this down in the audit, and the setup details are described on the pages about [Bitrix24](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/bitrix24/) and [amoCRM](/uslugi/vnedrenie-crm/amocrm/).

**Is there online repair booking with a website widget?**
Yes. We connect an online booking widget on the website and booking through messengers, so the customer picks a slot themselves, without a call. The request lands in the CRM right away and becomes a preliminary work order.

**Which integrations do you connect: telephony, 54-FZ checkout, 1C, SMS?**
We connect IP telephony with call capture and a record by number, online booking, inventory and parts catalogs, SMS and messengers for reminders, 1C, and an online checkout under 54-FZ. We assemble the set for your processes rather than connecting everything at once.

**How much does a turnkey CRM for auto service cost?**
Subscriptions for industry systems start from a few hundred rubles per month, while a one-time turnkey implementation on a platform runs from 50,000 RUB and grows with the depth of setup and the number of integrations. We name the exact estimate after a short process audit.

**How long does implementation take?**
A pilot and the initial setup fit within 7–30 days, which for most shops is 2–4 weeks to a working result. Complex implementations with integrations and 1C take 3–8 weeks. Training the team takes 2–8 hours.

**Can the customer and vehicle base be moved from Excel or an old program?**
Yes. Migrating the base of customers, vehicles, and history from Excel or a previous system is a standard part of implementation. We clean and consolidate the data before loading, so vehicle records do not duplicate and history is not lost.

**Do you work with only one platform?**
No. We are an independent integrator and select a system to fit your shop from industry and universal solutions. We do not sell one program, so we recommend what will actually cover your processes, not what is profitable for us.
