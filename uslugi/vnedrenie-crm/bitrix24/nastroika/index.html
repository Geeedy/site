Bitrix24 Setup for Your Department's Processes

Your Bitrix24 is already deployed, but it sits empty or runs on defaults. The pipelines do not reflect your sales stages, managers see other people's deals, robots stay silent, telephony and email live apart from the cards. We fine-tune the ready portal to fit the department's real processes and leave alone what already works. There is no need to redeploy or start from scratch; setup is configuration, not reinstallation.

What Bitrix24 setup includes

Bitrix24 setup is the configuration of an already deployed portal to fit the company's real processes. We define pipelines, fields, rights, robots, telephony, email and tasks so the department works inside the system rather than around it. This is not development and not reinstallation; the portal stays the same, and its logic changes to fit you.

Setup differs from two neighbouring tasks. Deploying and launching a portal from scratch is turnkey Bitrix24 implementation, and connecting the portal to external systems (1C, the website, a telephony provider) is Bitrix24 integration with 1C and telephony. Here we deal only with setup inside an already ready portal.

What we configure:

  • Pipelines and stages of deals to match your sales stages.
  • Required card fields so the data stays clean.
  • Access rights and roles for employees by visibility level.
  • Robots and triggers on the pipeline stages.
  • Simple business processes for approvals inside the portal.
  • Telephony, email and tasks right in the CRM cards.
  • Reports and kanban views tailored to the employee's role.

Setting up CRM pipelines and stages

The pipeline is the backbone of your sales department in CRM. We create deal categories for your product lines, split each into stages that match the real steps of the work, and set up the kanban so a manager moves a deal left to right without extra clicks. The crooked default pipeline is replaced with one that describes exactly your process.

Next we define required card fields so a deal cannot move to the next stage without a phone number, an amount or a source. Automatic actions are switched on at the stages, and the head of department gets views and filters for their role. The outcome is simple: a clear pipeline and clean data you can build reports and plans on. This configuration covers the "CRM setup" sub-intent without reinstalling the portal.

Access rights and employee roles

Rights in Bitrix24 CRM are configured by roles and access levels: own deals, department deals or all deals, with separate limits on export and deletion. We describe roles for your structure: manager, head of sales, director, and grant access so each person sees exactly their own area of responsibility.

The typical risk of an unprotected portal is simple: managers see and export the entire client base, then carry it off when they leave. We close visibility of other people's deals, limit rights to change stages and delete records, and turn off base export where it is not needed. Separating rights is the first barrier against client leakage and the first thing we check during a portal audit.

Robots and business processes of the portal

Robots and triggers fire automatically at deal stages without programming: they set tasks, send emails and notifications, change the owner. We configure them so the routine moves out of the manager's hands and into the portal. A deal moves to the "Invoice issued" stage, and the robot sets a task to check payment and reminds again in two days.

Beyond robots, we assemble simple approval business processes inside the portal (a discount request, a contract approval). An important note on the scope of the service: complex end-to-end sales automation as a separate project belongs to the sales automation service. Within setup, robots are an element of the pipeline configuration, not standalone development.

Telephony, email and tasks

The three tools without which a deal card stays half-finished, we connect and configure right inside the portal.

Telephony

Inbound and outbound calls, call recording and the pop-up client card are configured with the built-in Bitrix24 telephony. We switch on calls in the card, routing to the owner, and recording for quality control. Connecting an external provider or an IP-PBX is already an integration task, which we move to the Bitrix24 integration with 1C and telephony service.

Email

Through the "Mail" section we connect mailboxes and link emails to deals and contacts. Correspondence with a client gathers in that client's card instead of getting lost in a manager's personal inbox. We also set up email templates for typical replies.

Tasks

Task templates, robots that assign tasks, deadlines and execution control are configured through tasks and projects. We link tasks to deal stages so no stage hangs without an owner and a deadline. The manager sees overdue items in one list instead of gathering them from chats.

Bitrix24 setup for the department

One portal is configured differently for different areas, and there is no universal template here. A sales department lives in a pipeline with stages and scripts; it needs a fast card and nurturing robots. A service desk works by the logic of tickets and SLAs; it needs response times and escalation. Procurement and production run their own approval and status flows.

We configure categories and rights so each department sees its own pipeline and its own fields while the data stays in a single portal. Sales get deals and calls, service gets tickets and rules, the head of department gets consolidated reports across all areas. The configuration adapts to the type of department, not the department to a boxed template.

Setup stages and timelines

We run setup in five steps so the portal changes predictably and without downtime for the department.

  1. Audit. We look at the current portal and processes and record what runs on defaults and where losses happen.
  2. Pipeline and fields plan. We agree stages, required fields and views before making changes in the portal.
  3. Rights and roles. We set up access levels, deal visibility, export limits.
  4. Robots, telephony, email, tasks. We switch on stage automation and connect channels into the card.
  5. Testing and training. We verify on real deals, train the staff, and provide support through the first weeks.

By the benchmarks of Russian integrators for 2026, basic setup of a pipeline and rights takes 3–7 business days, while an extended one (robots, telephony, email, tasks) takes 2–4 weeks. The exact timeline depends on the number of pipelines and the depth of automation, and we name it after the audit.
