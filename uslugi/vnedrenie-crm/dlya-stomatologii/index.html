Dental CRM: Tooth Chart, Scheduling and Patient Recalls

In most dental practices, appointments live in a paper logbook, the treatment plan sits in the doctor's head, and patients disappear after the first visit. Chairs sit idle, no one reminds patients about hygiene, and the clinic pays for new patients instead of bringing back its own. A dental CRM keeps an electronic patient record with a tooth chart and treatment plan, shows chair and doctor load, and reminds patients about recalls on its own. We are an independent integrator: we pick a system to fit your clinic format and configure it around real dental workflows rather than selling a boxed product. This is part of our CRM implementation service, where the effect is measured before and after.

Who a dental CRM fits

A dental CRM is a system that keeps an electronic patient record with a tooth chart and treatment plan, chair and doctor scheduling, reminders about recalls and hygiene, and handling of the price list and insurance. Unlike a generic CRM with no medical specifics, an industry solution stores the dental formula, tooth statuses and a staged treatment plan right in the patient card (dental-cloud.ru, infodent.ru). It fits a private office with one or two chairs, a standalone dental clinic, and a small chain of locations.

By specialty, the solution equally covers therapy, prosthetics, implantology and orthodontics: each needs the dental formula, a staged treatment plan, and control over the patient returning for the next stage. If you run a multi-specialty medical clinic rather than dentistry alone, see CRM for a medical clinic: there segmentation follows general medicine, while here the focus is strictly on dental specifics.

What a CRM solves in dentistry

Without a system, a dental practice loses money quietly, because the losses do not show up in the revenue report. Forgotten recalls, empty chairs, and a treatment plan kept in one doctor's head appear on no list. Acquiring a new patient costs several times more than retaining a familiar one, so systematic work with the base and with recalls becomes a key source of clinic growth (vc.ru, retailcrm.ru). A dental CRM closes these gaps with a set of tools:

  • Tooth chart and treatment plan - the dental formula with statuses and a staged plan with pricing right in the patient card.
  • Scheduling and chair load - a schedule by chair and doctor, online booking and idle-time control.
  • Recall and hygiene reminders - triggers for hygiene every 6 months and for the next treatment stage.
  • Price list, insurance and private health insurance (DMS) - a price list with stages, plan cost calculation, invoices and work with insurers.
  • Patient base - a single history of visits, images and payments instead of a paper logbook and spreadsheets.
  • Integrations - links with the medical information system (MIS), X-ray, telephony and messengers.

It is exactly the combination "tooth chart and treatment plan + chair schedule + return reminders" that sets a dental CRM apart from a plain contact list.

Tooth chart and treatment plan in the patient card

The core of the industry solution is the electronic patient record built around the dental formula. The tooth chart records the status of each tooth: healthy, caries, filling, crown, implant, extracted. The doctor marks changes right on the diagram, and the picture of the whole mouth comes together in one place (dental-cloud.ru, klientiks.ru).

The treatment plan grows out of the tooth chart. It breaks down into stages with specific procedures, timelines and pricing, and the stages roll up into the patient's overall invoice (dental-cloud.ru, klientiks.ru). The doctor and the administrator see which stage is done and which is waiting to be booked, so the patient does not drop out of treatment halfway. Alongside sits the history of visits and images, so there is no need to dig up papers or re-question the patient before the next visit. The card thus becomes a single source of truth about the person rather than a set of scattered notes.

Chair and doctor scheduling and load

Scheduling in dentistry revolves not only around doctors but also around chairs. The CRM shows the load of each room and chair, procedure durations and idle time, so the administrator sees where there is a slot and where the load is already tight. Online booking and chair occupancy control raise room utilization and clinic revenue (klientiks.ru, infoclinica.ru).

The system helps distribute the load across doctors and fit slot length to the procedure type, so an implant and a routine cleaning do not take the same time in the grid. Online booking from the website and aggregators feeds into the shared schedule rather than living separately. If you want to fully automate booking in a messenger, that is handled by a chatbot for patient booking: it takes over time selection and confirmation, and the slot drops straight into the chair schedule.

Recall and hygiene reminders: reducing churn

The main source of growth for a dental practice is the return of a familiar patient, not just a new flow. The CRM runs trigger reminders that bring the person back for a scheduled visit and hygiene via SMS and WhatsApp (dental-cloud.ru, vc.ru). The set of triggers is usually as follows:

  • Hygiene every 6 months - a reminder to book a scheduled cleaning.
  • Recall by treatment plan - a nudge to return for the next stage already in the card.
  • Reactivation of "dormant" patients - a touch to those who have not come in a while, with an incentive to book.
  • Booking confirmation - a reminder of the date and time to cut no-show rates.
  • Post-visit review - a feedback request that also brings the patient back into dialogue.

Since retention costs less than acquisition, systematic reminders reduce churn and bring patients back for a recall instead of the clinic paying for a new one each time (vc.ru, retailcrm.ru).

Price list, insurance and private health insurance (DMS)

A dental CRM works not only with treatment but also with the money around it. A price list with treatment stages, automatic plan cost calculation, generation of invoices and acts, and accounting of payments under private health insurance (DMS) and insurance programs come together in one loop (klientiks.ru, infodent.ru). Integrations: MIS, telephony, messengers

The value of the solution unfolds in links with the clinic's other systems. The main loop is the medical information system (MIS) and X-ray or radiovisiograph: images and medical data feed into the patient card, and the tooth chart and medical record are kept by the industry MIS itself (infoclinica.ru, dental-cloud.ru). The second loop is telephony: IP telephony with call recording ties calls to the card, so the administrator sees the patient's contact history.

The third loop is communications: WhatsApp and SMS notifications, online booking from the website and aggregators all converge into one window. If you use amoCRM or Bitrix24 for the client funnel and requests, they cover calls and messengers, while the tooth chart and medical record stay with the MIS; the integration links the CRM funnel with the medical system (amocrm.ru, klientiks.ru). If you need broader AI clinic process automation, that is a separate service: here we are responsible strictly for the CRM built for dentistry.

Stages of CRM implementation in dentistry

Turnkey implementation is not installing a program but configuring it around your clinic's workflows. It runs in stages (infodent.ru, klientiks.ru):

  • Process audit - how booking, treatment and payments are set up, and what already exists in the logbook, spreadsheets and the old system.
  • Tooth chart and price list setup - the dental formula, statuses, treatment plan stages and price list tailored to your clinic.
  • Patient base migration - consolidating history from the logbook, Excel or a prior system with duplicate cleanup.
  • Training administrators and doctors - so the reception and the chair work in the system from day one.
  • Launch and support - starting on real patients and adjusting scenarios based on the first results.

We run the setup from dental workflows rather than from checkboxes in the interface, so the system solves clinic tasks right away instead of adding work.

How much it costs and why us

The final estimate is made up of three items: the license of the chosen CRM or MIS, the implementation work, and integrations with MIS, X-ray, telephony and messengers. We lock the exact figure after a short audit, because it depends on the number of chairs and doctors, the set of specialties, and the state of the current data. The benchmark for a decision is simple: implementation should cost less than the forgotten recalls and idle chairs cost the clinic over the same period.

There are several specialized solutions for dentistry in Russia: Dental Cloud, Infodent, Klientiks, Infoclinica, IDENT; for the client loop and funnel they are often linked with amoCRM or Bitrix24 (top107.ru, dental-cloud.ru). From our practice. The tooth chart and price list can be configured quickly, but getting the reception to keep everything in the system rather than in parallel in a notebook is harder.
