AI for Marketplaces: Product Cards and Infographics for WB, Ozon and Yandex Market

The product card decides whether a buyer sees your item or not. On Wildberries and Ozon almost all traffic comes from the platform's internal search (vc.ru), so the title, keywords and infographics drive both impressions and buyouts. We build an AI agent that creates product cards at scale across your catalog, prepares infographics, replies to reviews and keeps prices within the target range. This is not a one-off text generator but a working tool for a catalog of hundreds and thousands of SKUs.

What AI for marketplaces can do

AI for marketplaces is a set of AI agents that generate product cards, prepare infographics, handle reviews and reprice items on WB, Ozon and Yandex Market. Instead of assembling every card by hand in the dashboard, the seller gets a conveyor: product and attributes go in, a ready card draft comes out.

The agent covers four zones of a seller's routine: card content, visuals, reputation and price. Each of these used to be run by a separate person or contractor, and now a single process ties them together with your control at the finish. We cover broader automation scenarios in the AI agents for business section; here the focus is only on marketplace seller tasks.

Product card generation: title, description, attributes, SEO keywords

This is the core block of work. The agent takes the product name, category and attributes, adds high-frequency queries from the platform's internal search, and assembles the title and description by the marketplace's rules. You get a draft to edit, and final approval stays with you.

What the agent does at this step:

  • Title by the platform's formula: product type, brand, key features, without keyword stuffing.
  • Description with benefits and use cases, weaving in SEO keywords.
  • Attributes mapped to the category, so the card passes buyer filters.
  • SEO keywords from the internal search of WB and Ozon, not from Yandex and Google.

Scale is the main difference from manual work and from one-off free generators. The agent processes a batch of SKUs at once and keeps a single style across the whole catalog. Text generation is handled by generative AI for text and content, adapted to marketplace requirements.

AI infographics for product cards

Infographics on product photos are the selling images of a listing: bullets with benefits, sizes, composition and use cases placed over the shot. The agent prepares layouts using your brand template, you approve and upload them. Each platform has its own requirements for image sizes and counts, so templates are split across WB, Ozon and Market.

We describe deep processing and generation of the product images themselves on the AI product image processing page. In the card pipeline, infographics are the final layer over a finished photo, not a replacement for product photography.

Working with reviews and ratings

The agent gathers new reviews, tags sentiment and topics (quality, delivery, size), highlights recurring complaints and drafts replies in your tone of voice. You approve the templates, then routine replies go out automatically, while the agent flags complex cases and hands them to a manager.

Rating directly affects position and buyouts, and platforms reward a fast response to reviews. A steady flow of correct replies takes the load off the manager and keeps negativity from piling up. Similar scenarios for a website and messengers are covered by the AI assistant for routine tasks.

Price analytics and repricing

Repricing is the automatic re-pricing of an item against competitors within a set margin range. The agent tracks prices in your niche, keeps you within the margin limits and does not let you drop below the minimum. This helps win buyouts and get into promotions without a manual price review every day.

Monitoring follows the platform's rules rather than running blind: the agent accounts for commission, logistics and the minimum price below which you cannot sell. You set the strategy, the agent executes it daily and shows what changed and why.

Integration with WB and Ozon API

The agent works through the platforms' official interfaces. On Wildberries this is the seller dashboard API (seller.wildberries.ru): creating and updating cards, stock, moderation statuses, reviews. On Ozon this is the Ozon Seller API (docs.ozon.ru): cards, category attributes, prices and promotions, questions and reviews. Yandex Market connects through the seller API (yandex.ru/support/marketplace).

Bulk updates of hundreds of SKUs run on a schedule, without manually entering each field in the interface. The set of available fields differs across platforms, so a dedicated card template is prepared for each one rather than one text for all three.
