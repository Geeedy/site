Promo Website Development for a Campaign, Product Launch and Event

A promo website is needed when a campaign has a bold creative message and a hard deadline: a new product launch, an ad campaign, a contest or a brand anniversary. It is a temporary web project that lives for the run of the campaign and bets on emotion, not just on a request form. We build such sites richer than an ordinary one-pager, through animation, interactivity and storytelling, so the visitor walks through the product story and remembers the brand. This is one of the directions within website development: we pick the format for the task rather than selling one template for everything.

What a promo website is and why business needs it

A promo website is a bold temporary web project for a specific campaign, product launch or event, richer than a landing thanks to animation, interactivity and storytelling, and its goal is a wow effect and audience engagement. Unlike a permanent catalog or a corporate site, it tells the story of a single product or campaign more broadly and more emotionally than an ordinary landing page. The purpose of such a site is not to collect the maximum of requests head-on, but to bring a new brand or product to market, create an impression and draw the visitor into the story. That is exactly why a promo website is ordered for launches, sales and events where creativity and reach matter.

How a promo website differs from a landing page

A promo website and a landing page are often confused, but their tasks differ. A landing page is a permanent one-pager for a single goal, conversion into a request or a signup, with minimal navigation and everything subordinated to one action. A promo website is built for image, value explanation and engagement, it is larger in content and scenario, with section navigation, a product story, media and animation.

ParameterPromo websiteLanding page
GoalImage, wow effect, engagementOne conversion into a request
LifespanTemporary, for the campaign runPermanent, works for years
StructureSections, scenario, navigationOne screen flow for one offer
Animation and interactivityAt the core of the ideaMinimal, so it does not disturb the form
Target actionWalk through the story, engageLeave a request

If the task is only to collect leads for one offer, a landing page suits you better, it is cheaper and tuned for conversion. If you need a bold project for a campaign, you are on the right page.

When you need a promo website

A promo website is justified where the task has a deadline and a creative message. The main occasions:

  • Launch of a new product or brand to show value and create a first impression.
  • Ad campaign, sale or special offer to gather attention around the offer for the campaign run.
  • Event, contest or occasion an event page with participation mechanics and engagement.
  • Brand anniversary and image campaigns to tell the company story emotionally.

What all these tasks share is being temporary and betting on emotion. The success of a promo website is measured by engagement and reach, not only by the number of leads.

What promo website development includes

A promo website is made richer than a landing by a set of techniques that hold attention and turn a view into an impression.

  • Animation holds attention and makes the brand feel alive.
  • Interactivity engages through quizzes, scroll effects, configurators and calculators.
  • Storytelling leads the user through the product story from screen to screen.
  • Microinteractions respond to every move and make the site feel responsive.
  • 3D and WebGL deliver a wow effect and show the product in volume right in the browser.
  • Parallax creates depth through layered scrolling.

We pick the set for the idea and budget: somewhere neat animation is enough, somewhere a special project with unique mechanics is needed.

How much a promo website costs

The price depends on the complexity of the animations, the presence of 3D/WebGL and the uniqueness of the mechanics. Reference points for the Russian market in 2026:

  • Simple promo website basic design, up to 5-10 blocks, a form, responsive, no complex animations: from 40,000 to 80,000 RUB, from 1-2 weeks.
  • With animation and interactivity custom design, animations, calculators and quizzes, basic integrations: 110,000 – 300,000 RUB, 2-6 weeks.
  • Special project with 3D/WebGL unique mechanics, complex animation, non-standard UX: from 300,000 to 700,000 RUB and above, 4-6 weeks and longer.

The average budget of a creative promo website on the market stays in the 200,000 – 400,000 RUB range, while premium special projects reach 700,000 – 900,000 RUB and over a million. We name the exact estimate after a short brief on your campaign.

Promo website development timelines

The timeline is dictated by the campaign format. A simple promo on a site builder we assemble from 1-2 weeks, a site with custom animation and interactivity takes 2-6 weeks, and a complex special project with 3D/WebGL from 4-6 weeks and longer. For a campaign with a fixed start date we work backward from the deadline: we plan the stages so the site goes live by the campaign start, not after it. If time is short, we will honestly say how much animation is realistic to finish and propose a format that will ship on time.

How development goes

  1. Concept. We get to know the product, audience and competitors, and come up with the idea and visual language of the promo.
  2. Specification. We fix the structure, functionality and details of the animations and interactivity.
  3. Design. We draw the screens and the story walkthrough scenario.
  4. Coding and animation. We build the site and program the effects, interactivity and microinteractions.
  5. Integrations. We connect forms, CRM and analytics to measure reach and requests.
  6. Testing and launch. We check on devices and release the site by the campaign start.
