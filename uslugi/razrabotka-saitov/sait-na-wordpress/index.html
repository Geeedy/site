Turnkey WordPress Website Development

WordPress remains the most widely used content management system in the world, and it is a convenient choice for a content-driven business. We use it to build corporate sites, blogs and media, landing pages and catalogs, and we also migrate to WordPress what already runs on another platform. At the same time we take on what owners of such a site fear most: security, regular updates, speed and SEO. This is part of our approach to website development: not to hand over an "engine with an admin panel", but to deliver a site that brings in leads and does not break a month later.

Why business chooses a WordPress website

A WordPress website is a site built on the most popular CMS in the world, which, according to W3Techs (August 2026), powers 40.8% of all websites on the internet and 59.0% of those where a content management system is identified at all. Such a platform is chosen for corporate sites, blogs and media, landing pages and catalogs, where it matters to manage content yourself without a developer.

There are three practical reasons behind this popularity. The first is flexible content management: pages, articles, menus and blocks are edited from the admin panel rather than through code changes. The second is a huge ecosystem of themes and plugins that covers common tasks without building from scratch. The third is a mature SEO foundation: a clear structure, human-readable URLs and compatibility with search engines. The scale of the platform also means that a contractor or an employee for WordPress is easier to find than for a niche system.

Who WordPress fits, and who is better off on another platform

WordPress is not good for everything. It is more honest to name the limits up front, so you do not overpay for the wrong tool.

When to choose WordPress:

  • a content-driven corporate site with regular news, articles and cases;
  • a blog or media outlet with a large volume of publications and sections;
  • a landing page or service page that will later grow into a full site;
  • a catalog of products or services of medium scale without complex integration with accounting systems.

When another platform is better:

  • a quick landing page or MVP without code and support, which is more of a Tilda website;
  • a large catalog tightly bound to 1C, with high load and a Russian license, which is a job for a Bitrix website.

We work with all three platforms, so at a free consultation we recommend the one that solves your task cheaper, not the one that is more convenient for us to build.

What kinds of sites we build on WordPress

On WordPress we cover four main project types.

  • Corporate site. Services, cases, team, blog and lead forms. More about this format is on the corporate website page.
  • Blog or media. Sections, authors, tags, convenient editing and subscriptions. WordPress historically grew out of a blogging platform, so here it is especially strong.
  • Landing page. A single-page site for a service, product or ad campaign built for conversion.
  • Catalog. A showcase of products or services with filters and cards, when a full online store is not yet needed.

A full online store on WooCommerce we split out as a separate service: it has a different logic for payment, delivery and cart. Here the topic is content sites and showcases.

Security and updates of a WordPress website

The main fear of a WordPress owner is "it gets hacked". The truth is that most hacks come not through the core but through outdated plugins and themes. The core itself is patched quickly: on August 6, 2026 the WordPress 7.0.3 update was released, closing 12 vulnerabilities, including a critical XSS. Security rests on the discipline of updates, not on luck.

We build protection in layers. We update the core, plugins and themes on a schedule, not "when something breaks". We keep the plugin set minimal and take only trusted ones, because every extra one is a new entry point. We install a web application firewall (WAF) against common attacks and password brute-forcing. We set up regular backups so the site can be restored in minutes. Such a perimeter is part of our support, not a one-off service.

SEO and speed of a WordPress website

WordPress provides a strong SEO foundation: a flexible content structure, human-readable URLs, metadata control, an automatic sitemap and mature SEO plugins such as Yoast and Rank Math. But the foundation is a base, not a finished result. Rankings come from technical setup and regular work with content, so we build SEO in at the development stage rather than "bolting it on later".

The same logic applies to speed. A fast WordPress site is a consequence of setup, not of the mere fact of choosing the platform. Final performance is affected by the theme, the number of plugins, the hosting, caching and image optimization. We choose a lightweight theme, cut off excess plugins, enable caching, compress images and pick adequate hosting. As a result the site passes Core Web Vitals, not just "opens".

How development goes

We run the project through transparent stages, so you understand what you pay for and when you get the result.

  1. Brief and structure. We gather tasks, target queries and the page map of the future site.
  2. Design. We draw mockups of the key pages for your brand and approve them before layout.
  3. Development. We build the layout, set up WordPress, themes and a minimal plugin set, and connect forms.
  4. Content and SEO. We upload content and configure metadata, URLs and the sitemap.
  5. Launch and support. We move it to hosting, check speed and security, hand over access and take it into maintenance.

A simple landing page we build from 2 weeks; a corporate site takes longer depending on the volume. We name the timelines at the start and keep them.
